Custom Packaging FAQ

Common sourcing questions

What is the MOQ?

Standard custom packaging MOQ starts at 500 PCS. The exact MOQ can vary by packaging type, material, printing process and finish.

Can BestPackFactory make both boxes and pouches?

Yes. The site covers custom boxes, rigid boxes, folding cartons, flexible pouches, coffee bags, cannabis mylar bags, labels, paper bags, bottles, tins and printed accessories.

Can I get a dieline before production?

Yes. BestPackFactory can help with dieline, structure confirmation, artwork review and sample production before bulk manufacturing.
