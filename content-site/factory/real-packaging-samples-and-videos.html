How to verify us, or any packaging supplier

  1. Check the legal entity and certificate status on our certifications page, which marks each record as current, expired or pending.
  2. Ask for a live video call from the production floor, and name the machine or product you want to see.
  3. Order a physical sample of your own design before bulk production. A free dieline and artwork proof comes back within 24 hours.
  4. Agree the colour tolerance (ΔE ≤ 3.0 against the approved proof) and the inspection level (AQL 2.5 for major defects) in writing.

What we do not claim

We do not claim FDA approval. Our FDA 21 CFR 177.1520 report covers selected tests on one submitted bag sample only. Brand marks visible in videos are sample details, not client endorsements.
