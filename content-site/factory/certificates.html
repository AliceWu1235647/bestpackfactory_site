Why evidence status matters

Check the holder, date and scope

A buyer should match the legal entity and identifier on each document, check the issuing body's public register, confirm that the document is still valid and read its exact scope. A test report must also be matched to the submitted sample and intended use. This register therefore labels current management-system certificates, an expired HACCP record, a single-sample food-contact report and FSC records whose holder-to-factory relationship still needs documentation.

How scope is applied to an order

Certification covers the converter's system and the material classes it is registered for. It does not automatically extend to every product, or to a claim a buyer's own market requires. To know which documents can be supplied for your specification, send the intended use, destination market and required standard before sampling. We will confirm the applicable certificate and evidence for that exact combination — and state clearly when the certificate does not cover it.
