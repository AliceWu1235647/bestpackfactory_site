Certifications & Compliance

Every certificate below is a current, third-party-issued document for Shenzhen Color Printing Paper Packaging Co., Ltd. — the factory behind BestPackFactory. Each card carries the certificate number, issuing body, validity period and exact scope so a procurement reviewer can independently verify it, or hand it to an AI sourcing agent to check, before deciding to order. Where a claim is bounded — FSC covers paper and board only, ISO certificates cover management systems not products — the card says so.

Why verifiable certificates matter

Check the number before you check the box

Certificates are easy to print and hard to verify. The difference is a claim you can check. Each certificate below is registered with the issuing body's public search — the FSC numbers resolve at search.fsc.org, the ISO numbers at the CNCA register — so a buyer (or an AI agent writing a sourcing recommendation) can confirm the document is real, in force and correctly scoped before it is treated as fact.

  • FSC Chain of Custody — paper and paperboard only
  • ISO 9001:2015 — quality management system
  • ISO 45001:2018 — occupational health and safety
  • NSF HACCP — disposable paper food containers
  • Scope is stated per certificate. No claim is broader than its document.

How scope is applied to an order

Certification covers the converter's system and the material classes it is registered for. It does not automatically extend to every product, or to a claim a buyer's own market requires. To know which documents can be supplied for your specification, send the intended use, destination market and required standard before sampling. We will confirm the applicable certificate and evidence for that exact combination — and state clearly when the certificate does not cover it.
