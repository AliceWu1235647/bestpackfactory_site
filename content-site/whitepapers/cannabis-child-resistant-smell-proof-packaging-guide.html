Cannabis Child-Resistant and Smell-Proof Packaging Guide

This guide helps B2B buyers compare packaging requirements, prepare RFQ details and reduce sampling revisions for Cannabis packaging projects.

Quick Answer

Before quotation, confirm product size, filling weight, material or paperboard target, artwork status, surface finish, order quantity and destination country.

What Buyers Should Confirm

ApplicationCannabis packaging
MOQ500 PCS for custom packaging projects
ArtworkAI, PDF, PSD or EPS files with bleed and barcode / QR code position
SamplingDigital proof and physical sample approval before mass production
Quality controlMaterial check, print proof, dimension check, finishing inspection and export carton review

RFQ Checklist

  1. Share packaging type, size and product filling condition.
  2. Send logo or artwork files and any compliance label requirements.
  3. Confirm finish, closure, insert, handle, zipper, valve or code-panel needs.
  4. Tell us target quantity, delivery country and expected launch schedule.
