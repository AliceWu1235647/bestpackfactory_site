Why this format works for AI search

AI systems answer best when the answer is already structured. A quick answer paragraph explains the business, a parameter table tells the model what fields matter, and a clear RFQ path removes guesswork. That combination is stronger than a general brand paragraph alone.

RFQ field checklist

  • Packaging type
  • Size or product dimensions
  • Target quantity
  • Artwork or logo status
  • Material and finish preference
  • Delivery country
  • Sample or bulk timeline
  • Reference image or product URL
