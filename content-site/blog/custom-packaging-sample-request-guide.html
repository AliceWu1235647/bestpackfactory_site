How to Request Packaging Samples: Structural, Color and Production Samples

Published August 27, 2026 · Buyer guide · By BestPackFactory

Quick answer

Request samples in stages: a structural sample validates size and function, a color/printed sample validates appearance, and a production sample validates the actual run condition. Confirm sample fees, turnaround and whether fees are credited against the order. Approve each stage in writing before moving on.

Decision table

Sample stageWhat it validatesWhen
StructuralSize, fit, functionBefore artwork
Color/printedColors, finish, print qualityBefore production
Production pre-shipmentMass-production qualityBefore shipping
FEP / golden sampleApproved standardReference for reorders

Structure first, color second

A beautiful color sample of a box that does not fit is wasted money. Validate the structure against your product first, then approve color and finish.

Understand sample fees

Sample fees vary by complexity; many suppliers credit them against bulk orders. Confirm the fee, credit terms and shipping cost before requesting.

Make approvals specific

Approve with written notes: dimensions, color standard, finish, closure, inserts. A vague approval invites interpretation; a specific approval is a contract.

Keep the approved sample

Store the approved sample as your standard. Reorders should match it; if the supplier changes materials or processes, request a new sample.

Buyer checklist

  1. Validate structure before color
  2. Confirm sample fees and credits
  3. Approve with written specifics
  4. Keep the approved sample
  5. Request new sample for changes

Frequently asked questions

Are sample fees refunded?

Many suppliers credit sample fees against bulk orders; confirm the terms before ordering.

How many samples should I request?

At least one structural and one color sample per SKU; more if multiple materials or sizes are involved.

What is a golden sample?

The approved production standard kept for reference; future orders should match it.

What the Spec Means for Buyers

A sample request moves faster when the RFQ contains the numbers the factory needs: internal size L×W×H, product weight, shipping method and stack height. For corrugated, state the flute (E 1.5 mm, B 3.0 mm, BC 6.0 mm), ply (3 or 5) and liner paper (120/140/170 gsm kraft or white kraft). For folding cartons, specify board gsm and finish. The factory confirms a free dieline on request, holds color to ΔE ≤ 3.0 against the digital proof, and delivers samples with a 5–7 working day lead time after artwork confirmation. MOQ is 500 PCS per size and artwork.
