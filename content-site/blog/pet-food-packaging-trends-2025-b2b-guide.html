Pet Food Packaging Trends 2025: B2B Guide to Custom Bags & Pouches

Published June 2025 · 8 min read · By BestPackFactory

The global pet food market surpassed $115 billion in 2024 and continues to grow at 5–7% annually. As consumer spending on pet nutrition increases, the demand for premium, functional, and sustainable custom pet food packaging is accelerating. This guide covers the top trends B2B buyers should know when sourcing custom pet food bags and pouches in 2025.

1. Premiumization: Pet Food Packaging Goes Upscale

Pet owners are increasingly treating pets as family members — and they expect packaging that reflects premium product quality. Key premiumization trends:

  • Soft-touch matte lamination: Creates a tactile, luxurious feel that commands shelf premium
  • Spot UV on logos and icons: Creates contrast between matte and gloss areas
  • Metallic foil accents: Gold or silver hot foil stamping signals quality
  • Photography-quality photographic print: High-resolution pet and ingredient imagery on bag front
  • Extended panel design: Full-wrap printing that uses the entire bag surface including the bottom gusset

2. Sustainability-Driven Material Shifts

Regulatory pressure and consumer demand are pushing pet food brands toward more sustainable packaging materials:

3. Functional Packaging Features in High Demand

B2B buyers sourcing pet food packaging in 2025 should specify these functional features:

  • Resealable zipper: Essential for dry food bags. Press-to-close or slider options.
  • Easy tear notch: First-open convenience, especially for wet food pouches
  • Large format bags (10kg / 20kg): Growing segment for premium kibble and raw food brands
  • Stand-up bottom gusset: Flat bottom for maximum shelf stability and billboarding
  • Oxygen barrier: Multi-layer foil laminate to extend shelf life 12–24 months

4. Regulatory Compliance for Pet Food Packaging

Pet food packaging must comply with food contact material regulations in the target market:

  • USA: FDA 21 CFR (food contact materials)
  • EU: Regulation (EC) No 1935/2004 and 10/2011 for plastic food contact
  • UK: UK Food Contact Regulations (post-Brexit)
  • China: GB 4806 series for food contact plastic
