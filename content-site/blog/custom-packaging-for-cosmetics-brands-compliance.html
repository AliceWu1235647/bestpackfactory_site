Cosmetics Packaging Compliance: What Beauty Brands Should Verify

Published August 28, 2026 · Buyer guide · By BestPackFactory

Quick answer

Cosmetics packaging compliance starts with the material-contact picture and labeling: which materials touch the product, which labels and warnings apply in each market, and what documents you can request from the supplier. Confirm requirements for every destination with qualified compliance advice before printing, and keep material declarations with the order.

Understand the contact picture

Identify which packaging materials touch the cosmetic, for how long and at what temperature. Request declarations for those contact layers and confirm suitability with compliance advice.

Labeling is market-specific

Ingredient lists, warnings and mandatory statements vary by market and change over time. Confirm the label layout for each destination before printing, and plan the packaging so labels fit.

Batch codes and traceability

Cosmetics typically need batch and expiry information. Plan a durable printing area and confirm readability over the shelf life.

Build documents into the order

List the required declarations and records in the RFQ and PO, review them before mass production, and retain them with lot information.

Buyer checklist

  1. Map contact materials and uses
  2. Confirm labeling per market
  3. Plan batch and expiry areas
  4. Review claims wording
  5. Retain documents by lot

Frequently asked questions

Do I need documents for every material?

Confirm the status of every contact layer with compliance advice; declarations support your records.

Cosmetic claims are regulated; verify wording with qualified compliance advice.

Where do batch codes go?

On a durable, visible area; confirm readability over the shelf life.

What the Spec Means for Buyers

Cosmetics compliance starts with material declarations and print control. Custom cosmetic boxes use 1200/1500/1800 gsm greyboard wrapped in 157 gsm C1S art paper, 120 gsm specialty or kraft, with magnet options N35 Ø10×2 mm or 12×2 mm and pair position tolerance ±0.8 mm. Corner gap ≤ 1.5 mm, lid-base fit 1.0–1.8 mm. Printing is offset 1–6 colors with foil stamping die tolerance ±0.3 mm and color ΔE ≤ 3.0. Insert options include EVA foam, paperboard tray, molded pulp and PET blister tray. FDA-compliant ink/material statements and MSDS are available on request for the selected structure.
