Laminate thickness

Pet food bags commonly use PET/VMPET/PE with 120–180 µm total thickness depending on kibble size, fat content and filling weight.

Zipper choice

For 2 kg+ bags, heavy-duty 8–12 mm zipper or slider zipper is recommended for repeat opening and closing.

Drop test

A filled bag drop test at 1.0 m on six faces helps validate seal strength and bottom gusset durability before mass production.

Barrier and seal verification method

Method for Pet Food Packaging Buyer Guide: Specify the final laminate, specimen count, conditioning, temperature and humidity, test method, equipment, units and acceptance limits before comparing barrier or seal results.

  • ISO 2528:2017 specifies a gravimetric method for water-vapour transmission rate and states important sensitivity and material limits for that method.
  • ASTM F88/F88M-23 measures flexible-barrier seal strength and requires the support technique to remain consistent within a test series.

Evidence limit: A material name or generic target is not a batch result. Buyers should require a report for the final printed laminate that identifies the method, conditions, specimen preparation, results and acceptance limit.
