Custom Packaging Finishes: Gloss, Matte, Soft-Touch and Spot UV Explained

Published August 26, 2026 · Buyer guide · By BestPackFactory

Quick answer

Gloss lamination gives a bright, protective surface; matte gives a subdued, elegant look; soft-touch adds a premium velvety feel; and spot UV adds selective shine on matte. Match the finish to your brand, product and handling conditions, and approve a physical sample because feel and color change with each finish.

Feel is a brand decision

A soft-touch rigid box says luxury; a glossy carton says accessible retail. Handle samples of each finish with your product inside before deciding, because the feel is part of the unboxing.

Durability depends on use

Matte finishes can show scuffs and fingerprints in heavy handling; gloss protects better against abrasion. Consider how the box travels, sits on the shelf and is handled by consumers.

Color changes with finish

The same artwork looks different under gloss, matte and soft-touch. Approve color on the final finish, not on an uncoated proof.

Combine finishes for impact

Spot UV on matte, or foil plus soft-touch, creates selective highlights that guide the eye. Confirm the combined finish with a physical sample and check production lead time.

Buyer checklist

  1. Feel samples with product inside
  2. Match finish to handling conditions
  3. Approve color on final finish
  4. Test combined finishes
  5. Confirm lead time impact

Frequently asked questions

Which finish looks most premium?

Soft-touch and matte with spot UV or foil read as premium; confirm with samples.

Does matte show fingerprints?

Matte surfaces can show marks; consider gloss or a more durable matte grade for heavy handling.

Can I combine finishes?

Yes, combinations like matte plus spot UV are common; confirm with a physical sample.

What the Spec Means for Buyers

Finishes are the last 10% of packaging cost and the first thing customers touch. Matte lamination is the flat, anti-glare baseline; gloss lamination adds shine and pop; soft-touch film gives a velvet feel; spot UV adds contrast on matte; hot foil, embossing and debossing add logo depth. On rigid boxes, finishes sit on 157 gsm C1S art paper over 1200–1800 gsm greyboard; on folding cartons, on SBS 250–450 gsm. All are confirmed against the approved sample.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which finish feels most premium?

Soft-touch lamination combined with spot UV or hot foil is the standard premium feel.

What is the difference between gloss and spot UV?

Gloss laminates the whole surface; spot UV applies gloss selectively for contrast on matte.
