Custom Cigar Boxes Packaging: Design, Materials and Humidity Guide

Published August 21, 2026 · Buyer guide · By BestPackFactory

Quick answer

Cigar boxes are rigid or hinged-lid structures sized to hold 5-50 cigars in a divider tray, with a cedar-look or flocked interior that does not absorb or trap humidity unevenly. Note: the box is a presentation layer, not a humidor; humidity control belongs to a separate humidor or Boveda-style system. MOQ starts at 500-1,000 pieces, samples in 7-10 days, bulk 20-30 days, and confirm the divider cells are sized to ring gauge.

Sizing to ring gauge and length

Cell width should be cigar ring gauge plus 1-2 mm and cell length at cigar length plus 2-4 mm so cigars rest without rolling or compression. Confirm the tray material: greyboard dividers with flocking are standard; a cedar-veneer look (printed wood-grain paper or real cedar veneer) adds authenticity. Real cedar changes moisture behavior, so confirm with your logistics team how the box is stored before filling.

Humidity: what the box can and cannot do

A rigid box cannot regulate humidity on its own. If cigars ship inside the box, the distribution chain needs humidification or a humidity packet approved by your market; otherwise the box is a presentation layer only. Ask the factory to keep materials low-odor (uncoated interiors can smell) and request a VOC or odor check on the first sample, since off-odors are absorbed by cigars.

Interior finishes: flocking, velvet, wood-grain

Flocked EVA trays are the value option and grip cigars firmly; velvet-covered pads raise the premium feel; wood-grain liners (printed or veneer) give the classic humidor look. Confirm the interior color is printed or dyed to match brand guidelines and that white flocking does not yellow under display lighting. Request edge sealing on cut slots so flocking does not shed onto the cigars.

Branding, MOQ and lead time

Gold or silver foil stamping on the lid, embossed logos and a printed band are standard for cigar brands; keep foil artwork vector with at least 0.5 mm line weight. MOQ starts at 500-1,000 pieces depending on structure; samples take 7-10 days and bulk 20-30 days. For display-box lids that stay open, request a hinge-cycle test on the sample.

Buyer checklist

  1. Size divider cells at ring gauge plus 1-2 mm and length plus 2-4 mm
  2. Confirm interior material (flocked EVA, velvet, wood-grain) and edge sealing
  3. Request a low-odor / VOC check on the first physical sample
  4. Confirm humidity handling in the supply chain if cigars ship inside
  5. Keep foil and emboss artwork vector with ≥0.5 mm line weight

Frequently asked questions

Can a custom cigar box regulate humidity?

No: a rigid box is a presentation and transport layer, not a humidor. Humidity control belongs to a humidor or an approved humidification system in the supply chain, so plan storage and shipping accordingly.

What size should cigar box cells be?

Cell width at ring gauge plus 1-2 mm and cell length at cigar length plus 2-4 mm. This keeps cigars from rolling or compressing while staying easy to remove.

What interior material is standard for cigar boxes?

Flocked EVA trays are the most common value choice; velvet-covered pads and printed or veneer wood-grain liners are used for premium lines. Confirm edge sealing and an odor check on the first sample.

What the Spec Means for Buyers

Cigar boxes are a humidity and structure project. Rigid boxes from 1200/1500/1800 gsm greyboard wrapped in 157 gsm art paper or 120 gsm specialty paper provide the structure; EVA or flocked inserts hold cigars in place. Finishes of matte lamination, soft-touch, hot foil and embossing match the brand. Where humidity control matters, the insert and liner are confirmed with the approved structure. MOQ is 500 PCS per size and artwork, corner gap ≤ 1.5 mm, lid-base fit 1.0–1.8 mm.
