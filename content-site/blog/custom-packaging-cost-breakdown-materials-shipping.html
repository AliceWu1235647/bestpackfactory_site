Quick Answer for Sourcing

Total custom packaging costs are determined by raw material selection (Kraft vs. Cardstock), printing techniques (CMYK vs. Pantone), surface finishing (Spot UV, Foil), order volume (economies of scale), and shipping method (Sea vs. Air). Factory direct sourcing reduces middleman margins.

Key Buyer Checklist

  • Verify MOQ and material specifications.
  • Confirm dieline and artwork requirements.
  • Request lead time for production and shipping.
  • Validate barrier properties (for flexible packaging).

Shipping and landed-cost verification method

Method for Custom Packaging Cost Breakdown: Materials, Size, and Shipping: Record the packed outer dimensions, gross weight, carton count, pallet plan, route, service, Incoterms rule and included or excluded charges, then compare quotes on the same basis.

  • ICC Incoterms® 2020 allocates costs, risks and obligations between buyer and seller under 11 official trade rules.
  • UPS package dimensions and weight guide explains measurement, dimensional weight and carrier size or weight constraints.

Evidence limit: Carrier rules, divisors, surcharges and rates can change by service and contract. A page example is not a freight quote; the final landed cost must use current packed data and a dated carrier or forwarder quotation.
