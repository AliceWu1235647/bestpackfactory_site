Regulations decide the pack

CBD product rules vary by jurisdiction and change frequently. Confirm labeling, claims and packaging requirements with qualified compliance advice for every destination before specifying.

Protect the product itself

Gummies degrade with light, moisture and heat. Confirm the barrier structure and test product quality across the claimed shelf life.

Tamper evidence and dates

Confirm tamper evidence mechanisms that fit your line and a durable date and batch panel.

Regulated statements need clear, legible areas that cannot be damaged in handling. Include them in the artwork plan from the start.

What the Spec Means for Buyers

CBD gummies packaging needs an opaque, smell-proof structure with a reserved compliance area. The factory standard is PET 12µm / AL 7µm / PE 80µm (or MOPP 20µm / VMPET 12µm / PE 80µm), targeting OTR ≤ 0.5 cc/m²·day and WVTR ≤ 0.5 g/m²·day for the foil structure. A double-lock child-resistant zipper (ASTM D3475-style) or press-to-close zipper of 7–10 mm is available, seal strength is ≥ 35 N / 15 mm, and the design reserves space for warning icons, batch code, THC/CBD content area and a QR panel. The pack passes a vacuum leak test and a 20-cycle zipper test.
