Cardstock Product Boxes and Food Packaging Box Procurement Guide

A B2B sourcing guide for cardstock product boxes, food packaging box structures, retail cartons and printing parameters.

Quick Answer

Cardstock product boxes are cost-effective for retail products, cosmetics, snacks, supplements and food packaging. Buyers should define board thickness, coating, food-contact needs, print method, dieline, glue structure and logistics carton before pricing.

Parameter Table

StructuresTuck end box, auto-bottom box, sleeve, window box, hanger box, food tray sleeve and folding carton.
MaterialsSBS, C1S, C2S, kraft board, food-grade paperboard and recyclable cardstock.
Food packaging noteConfirm grease resistance, food-contact requirement, window film and coating before sample.
Print finishesOffset printing, matte/gloss lamination, aqueous coating, foil, embossing and spot UV.
Logistics noteFlat shipping reduces volume, but carton strength and folding accuracy must be controlled.

When cardstock boxes are the right choice

Cardstock product boxes are ideal when buyers need branded retail packaging without the freight volume of rigid boxes. They are especially useful for product launches, retail displays and food packaging projects.

Design to manufacturing workflow

The workflow includes structure choice, dieline, artwork, material confirmation, sample proof, print production, die-cutting, gluing, QC and flat export packing.

Procurement Checklist

  1. Provide product dimensions and filled weight.
  2. Confirm whether food contact or grease resistance is required.
  3. Select opening style and locking method.
  4. Ask for dieline with bleed, glue flap and barcode quiet zone.
  5. Confirm carton quantity, carton size and shipping method.

FAQ

What are cardstock product boxes used for?

They are used for cosmetics, supplements, snacks, retail products, food packaging and light gift items.

Can cardstock boxes be food safe?

Yes, if the paperboard, coating, ink and structure are specified for the intended food packaging use.

What is the advantage over rigid boxes?

Cardstock boxes are lighter, foldable and usually more cost-effective for retail runs.
