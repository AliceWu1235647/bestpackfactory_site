4. Valve Options: One-Way Degassing Valve

One of the most important features in a custom coffee bag is the one-way degassing valve. Freshly roasted coffee releases CO₂ continuously for days after roasting. Without a degassing valve, bags would inflate and burst. The valve allows gas to escape while preventing oxygen from entering — preserving freshness.

BestPackFactory supplies:

  • Standard 22mm one-way degassing valve (most common)
  • Adhesive-back or heat-sealed valve application
  • Optional screw cap spout for resealable pouches
  • Valve placement customization: front, back, or side gusset

5. Print Finishing Options

The surface finish of your custom coffee bags dramatically affects perceived quality and shelf appeal:

  • Matte Lamination: Premium, soft-touch feel. Popular for specialty and artisan coffee.
  • Gloss Lamination: Vibrant, shiny finish. Maximizes color saturation.
  • Soft-Touch Velvet: Ultra-premium. Used for high-end single-origin products.
  • Spot UV: Selective glossy coating on logo or key design elements.
  • Hot Foil Stamping: Gold, silver or holographic foil for luxury positioning.

  • ISO 2528:2017 specifies a gravimetric method for water-vapour transmission rate and states important sensitivity and material limits for that method.
