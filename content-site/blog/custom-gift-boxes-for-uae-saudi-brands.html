Custom Gift Boxes for UAE and Saudi Brands: Seasons, Arabic Artwork and Shipping

Written by Lisa Wu, Sales Manager & Packaging Project AdvisorPublished October 30, 2026Reviewed October 30, 2026

Lisa coordinates B2B packaging specifications, quotations, sampling and production communication with the BestPackFactory team.

Quick answer

For UAE and Saudi gift programmes, plan the box backwards from the season (Ramadan 2027 is expected to begin around 8 February 2027, subject to moon sighting), have Arabic text typeset by someone using right-to-left capable software and approved by a native reader on the physical sample, and choose materials that tolerate heat and humidity in transit and storage. Ship by sea to Jebel Ali, Jeddah or Dammam when time allows. Confirm customs, SABER and labelling requirements with your importer or broker, as of October 2026.

Plan backwards from the season

Gift box demand in the Gulf clusters around Ramadan, Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha, plus national dates such as UAE National Day on 2 December, Saudi Founding Day on 22 February and Saudi National Day on 23 September. Islamic dates move about 11 days earlier each year and are confirmed by moon sighting. Published forecasts put Ramadan 2027 starting around 8 February 2027, Eid al-Fitr around 9 or 10 March 2027 and Eid al-Adha around mid-May 2027.

Work backwards from the date the filled boxes must reach stores or clients: kitting and filling, inland delivery, clearance, sea or air transit, production, sample approval and artwork. Arabic proofing and calligraphy approvals often take longer than buyers expect, so start artwork early. If the timeline is tight, a common approach is to air a first quantity and send the balance by sea.

Arabic right-to-left artwork: how to avoid reprints

Arabic typesetting is where most gift box errors happen, usually because text passes through software or people that do not handle right-to-left script. Practical rules:

  1. Typeset in RTL-capable software. Arabic letters join and change shape by position. Layout software without complex-script support can show letters disconnected or reversed. Ask your designer to set Arabic in a layout tool with right-to-left support enabled, then send the factory a print-ready PDF.
  2. Outline or embed fonts after approval. The factory may not have your Arabic font. Send outlined text or embedded fonts plus a flattened reference PDF so the file cannot reflow.
  3. Never retype Arabic at the factory. Copying Arabic from a Word document or chat message into artwork is a common cause of broken letters. Only use text inside your approved artwork file.
  4. Decide the reading direction of the box. Arabic reads right to left. For a book-style magnetic box, decide which side the flap opens from, and lay out bilingual panels so the Arabic and English blocks each read naturally.
  5. Choose numerals deliberately. Decide whether to use Eastern Arabic or Western digits and keep them consistent across box, card and label.
  6. Check fine strokes under finishes. Calligraphy and small diacritics can fill in under hot foil or blind debossing. Confirm minimum stroke width on a physical sample; see artwork and dieline preparation.
  7. Proofread on the physical sample. A native Arabic reader on your side should approve the printed sample. The factory checks that print matches your file; it should not be relied on to proofread Arabic language.

Heat, humidity and handling

Containers and warehouses in the Gulf can get very hot in summer, and boxes move between air-conditioned spaces and humid outdoor air. That can affect greyboard flatness, glue lines, wrap paper adhesion and soft-touch surfaces. Ask for the adhesive and board choices to be confirmed for your shipping season, specify moisture protection in export cartons, and let cartons acclimatise before opening them for kitting. Check magnetic closures and inserts on the pre-production sample after a few days in your own storage conditions.

Food and fragrance contents

Where dates, sweets or chocolates sit directly on a tray, specify a food-contact suitable tray or a liner and ask what documentation can be supplied for it. For oud and perfume, send the exact bottle, cap and atomiser dimensions so the insert holds the bottle firmly; strong-smelling insert materials can conflict with fragrance, so ask for a sample insert. Shipping and clearance (as of October 2026)

  • Ports. UAE consignments commonly arrive at Jebel Ali. Saudi consignments commonly arrive at Jeddah Islamic Port on the Red Sea or King Abdulaziz Port in Dammam on the Gulf, with inland trucking to Riyadh.
  • Duty and VAT. Both countries apply GCC customs rules and charge import duty and VAT according to the tariff classification and value. Ask your broker for the current position for your HS code; we do not quote rates.
  • Saudi conformity. Imports into Saudi Arabia go through the SABER conformity platform. What is required depends on the product’s classification, so your importer should confirm the SABER requirement before the goods ship.
  • Documents. Expect a commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading and certificate of origin, and check with your importer whether any documents need legalisation or attestation.
  • Arabic consumer information. Consumer products sold in both markets commonly need Arabic product information. Decide whether it is printed on the gift box, on the product, or on a sticker.

What to send in your RFQ

  1. Box format, inside dimensions and the filled weight
  2. Insert or tray layout, with product samples or exact dimensions
  3. Arabic and English artwork as a print-ready PDF with outlined or embedded fonts
  4. Finishes: foil colour, embossing, soft-touch, spot UV
  5. Quantity per design (from 500 PCS) and any split delivery
  6. Destination city and port, preferred Incoterm and in-store date

Common mistakes

  • Arabic text retyped or pasted outside RTL-capable software.
  • Approving Arabic on screen only, not on the printed sample.
  • Starting artwork too close to Ramadan.
  • Ignoring heat and humidity when choosing board and adhesives.
  • Shipping to Saudi Arabia before the importer has confirmed SABER requirements.

Frequently asked questions

Can a China factory print Arabic text on gift boxes?

Yes, from a print-ready file typeset in right-to-left capable software, with fonts outlined or embedded. A native Arabic reader on your side should approve the printed sample.

When should I order Ramadan gift boxes for 2027?

Work backwards from your in-store date, allowing for artwork, Arabic proofing, sampling, production, shipping and clearance. Ramadan 2027 is expected to begin around 8 February 2027, subject to moon sighting.

Which port should gift boxes ship to for Saudi Arabia?

Jeddah Islamic Port and King Abdulaziz Port in Dammam are common entry points, with inland transport to Riyadh. Your forwarder chooses based on final delivery city.

Do I need SABER for packaging imported into Saudi Arabia?

Saudi imports go through the SABER conformity platform, and the requirement depends on the product classification. Ask your importer or broker to confirm before shipment.
