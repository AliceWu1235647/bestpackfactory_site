For first-time B2B packaging buyers, the concept of MOQ (Minimum Order Quantity) can be confusing. This guide explains everything you need to know about ordering custom packaging at MOQ 500 PCS — from pricing and artwork requirements to lead times and quality control.

1. What is MOQ and Why Does It Exist?

MOQ stands for Minimum Order Quantity. Manufacturers set a minimum order quantity because custom packaging requires setup costs — printing plates, color calibration, material cutting dies, and quality testing. These fixed costs are spread across the order quantity, so smaller orders are disproportionately expensive to produce.

At BestPackFactory, our MOQ is 500 PCS per unique design. This supports growing brands that need custom printing, sampling, quality control and project-specific compliance documentation without committing to a very large first order.

4. Artwork Requirements

To place a custom packaging order, you need:

  • Vector artwork file: Adobe Illustrator (.AI) or PDF with fonts outlined
  • Color mode: CMYK for process printing, Pantone codes for spot colors
  • Resolution: 300 DPI minimum for any raster elements (photos, textures)
  • Bleed: 3mm bleed on all sides
  • Safety zone: Keep critical text and logos 5mm inside the trim line

If you do not have design files, BestPackFactory can provide a free dieline template that you or your designer can use. We also offer a low-cost design service for brands without in-house creative teams.

6. Quality Control Process

Every order at BestPackFactory goes through a structured quality control process:

  1. Artwork pre-press check: Color accuracy, bleed, file integrity
  2. Print proof approval: First-off-press sample reviewed before run
  3. In-process inspection: Random sampling every 2 hours during production
  4. Final AQL inspection: AQL 2.5 standard for major defects before packing
  5. Packing and labeling check: Carton count, barcode scan, label verification

7. Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I order multiple SKUs at MOQ 500 PCS each?
A: Yes. Many brands order 3–5 different SKUs in a single production run. Each SKU requires its own MOQ 500 PCS.

Q: Can I combine different products to meet MOQ?
A: For the same product type, yes — for example, 300 PCS of 250g coffee bags + 200 PCS of 500g coffee bags. Contact us to discuss your specific situation.

Q: Do you offer samples without commitment to bulk order?
A: Yes. We offer pre-production samples (printed to your artwork) for a small sample fee that is credited against your bulk order.

Q: What payment terms do you offer?
A: Standard terms are 30% deposit before production, 70% balance before shipment. T/T, PayPal, and Alibaba Trade Assurance accepted.

Verification method and acceptance evidence

Method for Custom Packaging MOQ 500 PCS: What B2B Buyers Need to Know: Record the approved sample revision, packed condition, lot size, inspection level, defect classes, acceptance limit, conditioning, equipment and pass/fail rule before inspection.

  • ISO 2859-1:2026 defines AQL-indexed sampling schemes and acceptance or rejection rules for lot-by-lot inspection.
  • ASTM D5276-19(2023) covers free-fall drop testing of loaded boxes, cylindrical containers, bags and sacks as packed systems.

Evidence limit: These standards define repeatable methods, not a passing result for a BestPackFactory order. Release decisions require a project-specific inspection or test report tied to the approved sample and production lot.
