Custom Magnetic Gift Boxes for Cosmetics Brands: Buyer Guide

Published August 19, 2026 · Buyer guide · By BestPackFactory

Quick answer

For cosmetics brands, the standard magnetic gift box is a two-piece lid-and-base unit wrapped in 120-157 gsm art paper over 1.5-2.5 mm greyboard, with two neodymium magnets (typically 2 x 10 mm) set into the front flap. Order samples before tooling: most factories quote MOQ 500 pieces, sample lead time 7-10 days and production 20-30 days. Confirm magnet pull, insert type (EVA, flocked foam or paper) and lid closure tolerance of ±0.5 mm before mass production.

Start with structure and board spec

Cosmetics buyers usually start with a two-piece lid-and-base box because it is the most economical magnetic format. Specify greyboard at 1.5-2.5 mm (2.0 mm is the most common for 50-100 g products) and a wrap paper of 120-157 gsm art paper or 128-157 gsm coated paper for crisp CMYK printing. Ask the factory for the dieline with a lid depth of at least one-third of the base depth so the closure aligns cleanly.

Magnet strength: what to specify

Pull force is the number buyers should request in newtons. A 2 x 10 mm neodymium magnet typically delivers 0.8-1.5 N per closure and suits boxes under 300 g. For heavier or larger lids, ask for 3 mm thick magnets or two pairs per flap. Request a functional sample and check that magnets are fully concealed, since exposed or loose magnets are a common quality complaint in transit.

Inserts that protect the product

Cosmetics boxes need an insert that prevents movement during shipping. Flocked EVA foam (0.8-2.0 mm skin) is the default for bottles; paper honeycomb or greyboard trays work for lighter items and keep the box recyclable. Confirm insert depth tolerance of ±1 mm and ask for a drop-test report at 60-80 cm before approving bulk production.

MOQ, sampling and lead time

Entry-level MOQ is usually 500 pieces per SKU for lid-and-base and drawer formats; book-style and double-door boxes often start at 1,000 pieces. Tooling (dieline, magnets fixture, insert molds) is typically a one-time cost of USD 50-300. Plan 7-10 days for samples, 20-30 days for bulk after pre-production sample sign-off, plus 3-7 days for freight from China to US or EU ports.

Buyer checklist

  1. Confirm greyboard thickness (1.5-2.5 mm) and wrap paper gsm (120-157 gsm) in the RFQ
  2. Request magnet pull-force data in newtons or a functional closure sample
  3. Specify insert material and depth tolerance (±1 mm) for the product dimensions
  4. Ask for a drop-test report at 60-80 cm before approving bulk production
  5. Clarify MOQ per SKU, tooling cost split and sample vs bulk lead time

Frequently asked questions

What magnet size is standard in cosmetics magnetic boxes?

Most factories use two cylindrical neodymium magnets of roughly 2 x 10 mm per closure. Pull force depends on board thickness and wrap, so evaluate a physical sample rather than relying on a datasheet alone.

What is the typical MOQ for custom magnetic gift boxes?

500 pieces per SKU is the common entry point for lid-and-base and drawer formats; book-style or double-door structures often start at 1,000 pieces. Tooling is usually a separate one-time charge.

How long does production take after sample approval?

Allow 20-30 days for a standard 500-1,000 piece order after pre-production sample sign-off, plus 7-10 days for samples and 3-7 days for air or ocean freight depending on destination.

What the Spec Means for Buyers

Cosmetic magnetic boxes are a magnet-tolerance and insert project. Build on 1200/1500/1800 gsm greyboard wrapped in 157 gsm C1S art paper or 120 gsm specialty paper; magnets are N35 Ø10×2 mm or 12×2 mm with pair position tolerance ±0.8 mm. Corner gap ≤ 1.5 mm, lid-base fit 1.0–1.8 mm. Inserts include EVA foam, paperboard tray, molded pulp and PET blister tray. Offset printing runs 1–6 colors with foil die tolerance ±0.3 mm and color ΔE ≤ 3.0. MOQ is 500 PCS per size and artwork.
