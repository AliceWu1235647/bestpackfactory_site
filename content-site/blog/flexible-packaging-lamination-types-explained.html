Flexible Packaging Lamination Explained: PET, AL, PE, NY and VMPET

Published August 24, 2026 · Buyer guide · By BestPackFactory

Quick answer

Each film layer has a job: PET adds print surface and stiffness, aluminum foil blocks oxygen and light, PE provides sealing, nylon adds puncture strength, and metallized film (VMPET) gives barrier at lower cost than foil. Confirm the structure with the supplier against your product, shelf life and budget.

Decision table

Aluminum foilOxygen, light, moisture barrierHigher cost, crease risk

VMPETBarrier at lower costLess complete than foil

Every layer earns its place

A structure like PET/AL/PE balances print, barrier and sealing. Removing a layer to save cost changes performance; adding nylon improves puncture resistance for sharp products. Discuss the trade-offs with your product in mind.

Barrier claims need testing

Oxygen transmission rate (OTR) and water vapor transmission rate (WVTR) define barrier performance, but only mean something when matched to your product and shelf-life target. Request measured values and validate with a storage test.

Crease and flex risk

Foil structures can crease and develop pinholes in flexing and shipping. For products with long distribution, confirm flex-crack resistance or consider alternative barrier layers.

Sealing is part of the structure

The sealant layer and seal window determine package integrity. Confirm seal strength at your filling temperature and line speed, and test the complete pack.

Buyer checklist

  1. Map layers to product needs
  2. Request OTR and WVTR values
  3. Test flex and crease behavior
  4. Confirm seal performance
  5. Validate with a storage test

Frequently asked questions

Why is foil used in pouches?

Aluminum foil provides a strong barrier to oxygen, light and moisture, which protects sensitive products.

Is VMPET as good as foil?

VMPET offers good barrier at lower cost but is generally less complete than foil; match it to your product and shelf life.

What does nylon add?

Nylon improves puncture and abuse resistance for sharp or heavy products, at higher cost.

What the Spec Means for Buyers

Lamination types map to barrier and cost. Clear structures suit short-shelf-life dry goods; metallized (VMPET) adds an economical oxygen barrier; full foil laminates give the highest, most predictable barrier; PE-based structures prioritize recyclability. Frequently Asked Questions

When is foil lamination worth the cost?

Foil is worth it when oxygen and moisture sensitivity is high — coffee, pet food with high fat, and long-shelf-life products.

Are PE-based structures recyclable?

PE-based mono-material structures are the recyclable-oriented option; the compliance review confirms the exact build for your market.
