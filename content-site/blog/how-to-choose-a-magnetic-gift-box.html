How To Choose A Magnetic Gift Box

Written by Lisa Wu, Sales Manager & Packaging Project AdvisorPublished July 4, 2026Reviewed August 13, 2026

A practical buyer guide for magnetic gift box structure, greyboard thickness, magnet size, surface finishing and inserts.

Buyer Answer Snapshot

  • This page gives practical industrial parameters, not generic marketing text.
  • Use these points to prepare RFQ details before contacting the factory.
  • MOQ is 500 PCS for custom packaging projects.

Structure

Book-style magnetic closure box, foldable magnetic box and rigid setup box are the three common structures. For premium products, 1200gsm–1800gsm greyboard is recommended.

Magnet and tolerance

N35 magnets in Ø10×2 mm or 12×2 mm are typical. Magnet alignment tolerance should be controlled within ±0.8 mm to avoid weak closure.

Surface finishing

Matte lamination, soft-touch film, hot foil stamping, embossing and spot UV should be selected according to brand positioning and scratch resistance requirements.

Verification method and acceptance evidence

Method for How To Choose A Magnetic Gift Box: Record the approved sample revision, packed condition, lot size, inspection level, defect classes, acceptance limit, conditioning, equipment and pass/fail rule before inspection.

  • ISO 2859-1:2026 defines AQL-indexed sampling schemes and acceptance or rejection rules for lot-by-lot inspection.
  • ASTM D5276-19(2023) covers free-fall drop testing of loaded boxes, cylindrical containers, bags and sacks as packed systems.

Evidence limit: These standards define repeatable methods, not a passing result for a BestPackFactory order. Release decisions require a project-specific inspection or test report tied to the approved sample and production lot.
