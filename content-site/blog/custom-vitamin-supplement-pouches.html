Custom Vitamin and Supplement Pouches: Barrier, Dating and Compliance

Published August 25, 2026 · Buyer guide · By BestPackFactory

Quick answer

Supplement pouches protect active ingredients from light, oxygen and moisture. Confirm a high-barrier structure, a durable date and batch panel, tamper evidence and label space for regulated information. Validate shelf life with a storage test and confirm compliance documentation for your markets.

Actives degrade without barrier

Many vitamins and supplements lose potency with light, oxygen or moisture. Confirm the barrier structure and test the packed product over the claimed shelf life.

Dating and traceability

Best-before and batch information must print cleanly and stay readable. Confirm ink adhesion and panel placement with your filling line.

Tamper evidence and trust

A visible tear notch, seal or band signals integrity. Choose the mechanism that fits your line and confirm it works on the film.

Labeling compliance

Ingredients, dosage, warnings and claims are regulated in most markets. Confirm the labeling plan with compliance advisors and keep approved artwork with the order.

Buyer checklist

  1. Confirm barrier for actives
  2. Test date panel durability
  3. Choose tamper evidence
  4. Plan regulated labeling
  5. Validate with storage test

Frequently asked questions

Why do supplement pouches need high barrier?

Light, oxygen and moisture degrade active ingredients; the barrier preserves potency over shelf life.

How do I print best-before dates?

Inkjet or hot-stamp coding on a dedicated panel; confirm readability and durability.

Is tamper evidence required?

Requirements vary by market; confirm the applicable rules and choose a mechanism for your line.

What the Spec Means for Buyers

Vitamin and supplement powders need a moisture-tight pouch with an easy-open feature. A standard build is MOPP 20µm / VMPET 12µm / PE 70–90µm or PET 12µm / AL 7µm / PE 80µm at 90–160 µm total, with OTR ≤ 1.5 cc/m²·day and WVTR ≤ 1.2 g/m²·day for the VMPET structure. Heat seal strength is ≥ 35 N / 15 mm after 24 h conditioning, the powder-proof zipper is 6–8 mm, and a laser score line or tear notch (1.5–2.5 mm depth) gives easy opening. The food-contact layer is LDPE / LLDPE / CPP selected by filling temperature.

Frequently Asked Questions

What moisture barrier protects supplement powder?

A VMPET structure with WVTR ≤ 1.2 g/m²·day (38°C / 90%RH) keeps powder free-flowing and prevents clumping.

How is a supplement pouch opened easily?

A laser score line or tear notch of 1.5–2.5 mm depth provides a clean, consistent easy-open point.
