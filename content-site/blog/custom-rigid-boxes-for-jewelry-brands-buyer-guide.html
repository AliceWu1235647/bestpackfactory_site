Custom Rigid Boxes for Jewelry Brands: The Complete Buyer Guide

Published August 19, 2026 · Buyer guide · By BestPackFactory

Quick answer

Jewelry brands choose rigid boxes when the unboxing moment must justify the retail price. Specify 2.0-2.5 mm greyboard wrapped in 120-157 gsm paper, with flocked EVA or velvet-covered inserts cut to the exact item footprint. Typical MOQ is 500-1,000 pieces per SKU, samples in 7-10 days and bulk production in 20-30 days. Always request a pre-production sample to check insert fit, magnet alignment and hinge behavior before mass production.

Choose the format by presentation

Hinged-lid boxes open like a book and keep lid and base permanently aligned, which suits necklaces and rings displayed on a pad. Two-piece lid-and-base boxes are cheaper to tool and allow faster line packing, but the lid can shift in transit if magnets are weak. Drawer boxes add a deliberate slide-and-reveal moment that retailers and gifting customers respond to; expect a slightly higher unit price for the sleeve.

Linings: velvet, flocking and PU

Jewelry requires soft, non-abrasive surfaces. Flocked EVA foam (0.8-2.0 mm) is the workhorse for slots and channels; velvet-covered greyboard pads give a deeper luxury feel but add cost and a manual covering step. For silver and gold-plated items, avoid uncoated kraft or acidic boards in direct contact and request pH-neutral tissue or flocking to reduce tarnishing risk.

Sizes, tolerances and tooling

State inner dimensions with a product footprint plus 1-2 mm clearance per side, and insert depth that matches the item profile. Tolerance on cut slots should be ±0.5-1.0 mm. One-time tooling (steel-rule dies for inserts, magnets fixture) typically runs USD 50-250. Ask the factory to photo-document the fit of your actual product in the pre-production sample.

MOQ and lead times

500 pieces per SKU is the realistic floor for simple two-piece formats; hinged and drawer styles usually quote from 1,000 pieces. Sample lead time is 7-10 days, bulk 20-30 days after approval. If you need display trays with foam pads, add 3-5 days for the pad-lamination step. Reserve 2-3 weeks for ocean freight when budgeting the launch.

Buyer checklist

  1. Confirm rigid format (hinged, lid-and-base or drawer) and greyboard thickness
  2. Specify lining type: flocked EVA, velvet or PU, with slot tolerance ±0.5-1.0 mm
  3. Provide actual product for pre-production sample fit check
  4. Request pH-neutral tissue or flocking for silver and gold-plated items
  5. Confirm MOQ per SKU, tooling cost and sample vs bulk lead time

Frequently asked questions

Which rigid box format is best for necklaces?

A hinged-lid box with a velvet or flocked pad lets the chain lie flat without tangling and keeps lid and base aligned. Add a small slot or channel cut to the clasp so the piece stays in place.

What lining is safest for silver jewelry?

Flocked EVA or velvet-lined pads are the safest common choices. Ask for pH-neutral tissue or lining and avoid raw kraft in direct contact, which can accelerate tarnishing on silver and gold-plated finishes.

What MOQ should a jewelry brand expect for rigid boxes?

Simple two-piece formats start at 500 pieces per SKU; hinged-lid and drawer boxes usually require 1,000 pieces. Budget for one-time tooling of roughly USD 50-250 per design.

What the Spec Means for Buyers

Jewelry rigid boxes are a cavity-precision product. The box uses 1200/1500/1800 gsm greyboard wrapped in 157 gsm C1S art paper, 120 gsm specialty or kraft; the jewelry cavity is die-cut in EVA foam (20–60 density) or flocked trays from your product dimensions. Corner gap ≤ 1.5 mm and lid-base fit 1.0–1.8 mm. Finishes include matte lamination, soft-touch, hot foil, embossing and debossing. Each box passes a 24 h static load test. MOQ is 500 PCS per size and artwork.

Frequently Asked Questions

What cavity material suits jewelry?

Flocked trays for a soft presentation or EVA foam at 20–60 density for firm, secure cavities.

What is the standard finish stack for jewelry boxes?

Soft-touch or matte lamination with hot foil logo and optional embossing.
