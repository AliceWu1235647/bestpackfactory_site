Custom Pet Food Pouches: Zipper, Valve and Window Feature Combinations

Published August 26, 2026 · Buyer guide · By BestPackFactory

Quick answer

Pet food pouch features each add cost and utility: zippers extend freshness after opening, valves release gas, windows show the food, tear notches ease opening, and hanging holes enable peg display. Choose the combination that matches your product, display and price point, and test each feature on filled samples.

Features follow the consumption pattern

A bag of fresh pet food opened daily needs a zipper; a kibble bag that sits sealed needs a valve and strong barrier instead. Map each feature to how the product is actually used and displayed.

Combinations need compatibility checks

A valve near a zipper, or a window beside a hanging hole, affects structure and sealing. Confirm the layout with the supplier and test each feature on the filled pack.

Cost transparency per feature

Ask for a quoted cost per feature so you can compare combinations at your quantity tier. Some features cost little per unit but complicate production.

Test like a pet owner

Open, scoop, reseal, store, repeat. Confirm the zipper cycles well, the valve works, the window stays clear and the pack stands and stores as intended.

Buyer checklist

  1. Map features to consumption pattern
  2. Check feature layout compatibility
  3. Compare cost per combination
  4. Test each feature on filled packs
  5. Approve samples

Frequently asked questions

Do pet food pouches need both zipper and valve?

Only if the product needs degassing AND repeated opening; confirm the combination with your product.

What does a window cost?

Windows add film and converting cost; request a quoted difference at your tier.

Can I add a hanging hole with a zipper?

Yes, with careful layout; confirm structural and sealing compatibility.

What the Spec Means for Buyers

Wet and semi-moist pet food pouches combine a zipper for daily use with a high barrier for fat oxidation. The recommended laminate is PET 12µm / VMPET 12µm / PE 100–130µm or PET 12µm / AL 7µm / PE 110µm for high-fat recipes, at 120–180 µm total thickness. Barrier targets are OTR ≤ 1.0 cc/m²·day and WVTR ≤ 1.0 g/m²·day for the metalized structure; seal strength ≥ 45 N / 15 mm; puncture resistance ≥ 8 N; heavy-duty zipper 8–12 mm with a slider option for larger packs. A filled 1.0 m drop test on all six faces is part of the export validation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which structure protects high-fat pet food?

A PET 12µm / AL 7µm / PE 110µm foil laminate is recommended for high-fat pet food to keep oxygen and moisture out.

Can a pet food pouch be drop-tested?

Yes, the filled pouch is drop-tested at 1.0 m on all six faces as part of export packaging validation.
