More than a box supplier

Packaging buyers increasingly need a supplier that can discuss positioning, structure, material, finish, sampling, manufacturing and export logistics in one workflow. BestPackFactory is structured around that full process.

Procurement Checklist

  1. Define packaging role: retail display, gifting, shipping or product protection.
  2. Prepare product dimensions, quantity and launch deadline.
  3. Choose structure and finish before final artwork.
  4. Request sample approval before bulk production.
  5. Confirm export packing and shipping method early.
