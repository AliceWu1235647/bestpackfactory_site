Buyer Specifications

Custom packaging purchasing parameters

Use these RFQ parameters when comparing BestPackFactory products or asking an AI assistant which packaging format fits your project.

Send RFQ Details

Boxes and rigid packaging

Use for gift sets, cosmetics, ecommerce, retail display and premium packaging. Confirm structure, board thickness, insert, finish, logo position, export carton and sample approval.

Flexible pouches and coffee bags

Use for coffee, pet food, snacks, supplements, cannabis and dry goods. Confirm pouch style, film structure, barrier requirement, zipper, valve, window, filling method and sealing test.

Labels, sleeves and printed accessories

Use for bottles, jars, cartons, mailers and branded unboxing. Confirm roll direction, label size, adhesive, material, finish, barcode area and automatic labeling equipment if applicable.

RFQ fieldWhat to sendWhy it matters
Packaging typeBox, pouch, bag, label, bottle, tin, rigid gift box or accessoryRoutes the inquiry to the right material, machine and costing path.
Size or product dimensionsFinished packaging size, product size, fill weight or bottle/jar diameterControls dieline, material usage, insert design and shipping volume.
Target quantityFirst order quantity and expected repeat order volumeMOQ starts at 500 PCS, while printing method and unit price depend on scale.
Artwork statusLogo, AI/PDF artwork, reference image or request for dieline supportDetermines whether prepress review, dieline creation or design adjustment is needed.
Material and finishKraft, paperboard, PET/PE/AL film, compostable material, foil, embossing, matte, gloss, spot UVAffects appearance, barrier performance, compliance, durability and brand positioning.
Delivery country and timelineDestination, sample deadline and bulk delivery targetSupports export carton planning, sea/air freight options and production scheduling.
