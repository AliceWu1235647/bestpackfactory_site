Should food packaging use PE, PLA or water-based coating?

Should food packaging use PE, PLA or water-based coating? B2B procurement answer for custom food packaging: MOQ, materials, samples, QC, RFQ details and related product links.

Quick Answer

PE is common and moisture resistant, PLA supports compostable positioning where documented, and water-based coatings can support grease resistance with different limits.

What buyers should decide first

PE is common and moisture resistant, PLA supports compostable positioning where documented, and water-based coatings can support grease resistance with different limits. For a reliable quote, the buyer should define coating target, heat, moisture, compostability claim and disposal route before comparing suppliers or approving a sample.

BestPackFactory treats this as a B2B RFQ question, not a retail checkout item. The factory quote depends on size, material, printing, finish, quantity, carton packing and destination country.

Material, sample and QC points

For custom food packaging, common material and feature choices include SBS board, kraft paper, PE coating, PLA coating, water-based coating, PET/PE laminate, CPP sealant and grease-resistant paper. The safest sample approval checks food contact side, odor, grease resistance, moisture behavior, heat response, print rub, stacking and carton packing.

sample the coating with real food conditions This keeps the project tied to real production behavior instead of only a mockup or reference photo.

RFQ Checklist

  1. Send product type, dimensions, fill weight or capacity, and target use case.
  2. Confirm coating target, heat, moisture, compostability claim and disposal route.
  3. Share artwork status, logo files, barcode or QR code needs, and language versions.
  4. Request 500, 1000 and 3000 PCS tiers when comparing launch and reorder cost.
  5. Ask for sample time, bulk lead time, packed carton dimensions and shipping options.

Common Mistake to Avoid

making compostable claims without proof. This usually creates misleading price comparison or sample changes later.
