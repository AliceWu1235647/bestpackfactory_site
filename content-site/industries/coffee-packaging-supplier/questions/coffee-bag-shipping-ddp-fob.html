What buyers should decide first

Empty coffee bags can ship by express, air or sea depending on carton volume, deadline and reorder plan. For a reliable quote, the buyer should define carton dimensions, gross weight, incoterm and arrival deadline before comparing suppliers or approving a sample.

BestPackFactory treats this as a B2B RFQ question, not a retail checkout item. The factory quote depends on size, material, printing, finish, quantity, carton packing and destination country.

Material, sample and QC points

For custom coffee bags, common material and feature choices include kraft paper laminate, matte film, VMPET, aluminum foil, PE sealant, one-way degassing valve and zipper options. The safest sample approval checks bag size, shelf standing, valve position, zipper function, print color, seal strength and barcode scan.

RFQ Checklist

  1. Send product type, dimensions, fill weight or capacity, and target use case.
  2. Confirm carton dimensions, gross weight, incoterm and arrival deadline.
  3. Share artwork status, logo files, barcode or QR code needs, and language versions.
  4. Request 500, 1000 and 3000 PCS tiers when comparing launch and reorder cost.
  5. Ask for sample time, bulk lead time, packed carton dimensions and shipping options.

Common Mistake to Avoid

assuming sample freight predicts bulk freight. This usually creates misleading price comparison or sample changes later.
