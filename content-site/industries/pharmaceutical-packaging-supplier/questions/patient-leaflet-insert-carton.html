What buyers should decide first

Leaflets change inner clearance, closing behavior, carton bulge and pack-out process. For a reliable quote, the buyer should define leaflet folded size, insert position and product orientation before comparing suppliers or approving a sample.

BestPackFactory treats this as a B2B RFQ question, not a retail checkout item. The factory quote depends on size, material, printing, finish, quantity, carton packing and destination country.

Material, sample and QC points

For pharma folding cartons, common material and feature choices include SBS board, C1S board, ivory board, offset printing, varnish, tamper-evident structure, GS1 DataMatrix and batch-code panels. The safest sample approval checks carton fit, code readability, quiet zone, fold accuracy, glue strength, artwork version and carton count.

RFQ Checklist

  1. Send product type, dimensions, fill weight or capacity, and target use case.
  2. Confirm leaflet folded size, insert position and product orientation.
  3. Share artwork status, logo files, barcode or QR code needs, and language versions.
  4. Request 500, 1000 and 3000 PCS tiers when comparing launch and reorder cost.
  5. Ask for sample time, bulk lead time, packed carton dimensions and shipping options.

Common Mistake To Avoid

approving carton empty without leaflet. This usually creates misleading price comparison or sample changes later.
