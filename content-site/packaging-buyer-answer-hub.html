kraft coffee pouch vs foil pouch

Should coffee brands choose kraft paper pouch or foil pouch?

Kraft pouches support natural brand positioning, but still need an inner barrier for coffee freshness. Foil pouches usually provide stronger oxygen, moisture and aroma protection for longer shelf life.

flat bottom bag vs stand up pouch

When should buyers choose flat bottom bags instead of stand up pouches?

Flat bottom bags are better for premium shelf display, heavier fill weights and large printable panels. Stand up pouches are often more flexible and cost-efficient for smaller launches.

shipping custom packaging from China

Should buyers choose DDP, FOB, air freight or sea freight for custom packaging?

DDP is simpler for delivered landed-cost planning, FOB gives experienced importers freight control, air freight helps urgent launches, express suits samples and sea freight is usually best for large packaging volume.
