Custom Non-Woven Bags

Printed non-woven bags (also written non woven or nonwoven) for retail stores, trade shows, events, promotions and reusable shopping. Non-woven bag fabric is usually made from polypropylene (PP) fibres bonded into a sheet rather than woven, which is why it feels light and slightly papery. It is a plastic-based material, not paper and not cotton. For a woven fabric bag, see custom cotton tote bags; for a pouch that closes with a cord, see custom drawstring bags; the cotton tote, non-woven and drawstring bag selection guide compares all three.

Non-Woven Bag Structures and Options

This comparison explains common non-woven bag choices in the market. The fabric, construction and final specification for your order are confirmed in the quotation and on a physical sample.

MaterialNon-woven PP fabric is made by bonding polypropylene fibres into a sheet. Fabric weight is stated in gsm and is chosen from the bag size and load; the weight for your order is confirmed in the quotation.
Laminated or plainA laminated bag has a thin film bonded to the outer face, giving a smoother surface for detailed full-color graphics and better splash resistance. A plain (non-laminated) bag keeps the fabric texture and usually carries simpler solid-color print.
Bag shapeFlat bags suit documents and light giveaways. Bags with side and bottom gussets stand open and hold boxes, food containers and bulkier retail goods, as in the image above.
HandlesLoop handles are sewn or heat-bonded to the body, sometimes with a reinforcement patch. Die-cut handles are punched directly into the bag. Handle length decides hand carry or shoulder carry.
Seams and constructionSeams can be stitched or ultrasonically bonded. Stitched seams and reinforced handle joints are typical where heavier loads are expected; the construction for your order is confirmed in the quotation and checked on a sample.
PrintingPlain fabric is commonly screen printed in one to a few spot colors; full-color photographic designs are usually printed on the laminating film. The printing method is confirmed in the quotation.
PackingBags ship flat, bundled and cartoned. Bundle count and carton size are confirmed with the quotation.

FAQ

Custom Non-Woven Bag Questions

What is the difference between laminated and non-laminated non-woven bags?

A laminated bag has a thin film bonded to the fabric surface, which gives a smoother face for detailed full-color graphics and better resistance to splashes. A non-laminated bag keeps the soft fabric texture and is usually printed with simpler solid-color artwork. Tell us your artwork and how the bag will be used, and the option is confirmed in the quotation.

How are the handles attached?

Handles are commonly stitched or heat-bonded to the bag body, sometimes with a reinforcement patch at the joint, or cut directly into the bag as a die-cut handle. The handle construction for your order is confirmed in the quotation and checked on a sample.

How is the load capacity verified?

Load depends on the fabric weight, bag size, seam type and handle joint. Tell us what goes in the bag and its weight, and the filled bag is checked on a sample. We do not state a fixed load rating without that test.

Can non-woven bags be made to my size and color?

Yes. Bag size, fabric color, handle color and print are specified per order. Send your target size or a sample bag with Pantone references, and the specification is confirmed in the quotation.

Are non-woven bags plastic-free?

No. Non-woven bag fabric is usually made from polypropylene, which is a plastic, and laminated bags add a plastic film. We do not describe non-woven bags as plastic-free, biodegradable or compostable. If your market has rules for carrier or reusable bags, check them before ordering and tell us the requirement.
