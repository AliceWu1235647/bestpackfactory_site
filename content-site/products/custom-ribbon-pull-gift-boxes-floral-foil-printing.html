How to customize and approve this packaging

Start with the finished packaging size or the maximum dimensions and weight of every packed item. Specify the opening style, paper appearance, logo process, floral artwork, ribbon, insert and expected order quantity. BestPackFactory then prepares the structural and artwork workflow for project review.

Approve the physical sample with the intended products or accurate dummies. Check lid or drawer movement, corner quality, insert retention, finger access, artwork alignment, foil position, embossing detail and export-carton protection before bulk production.

Quote-ready RFQ checklist

  1. Finished package size or every packed product's dimensions and weight.
  2. Order quantity and target delivery schedule.
  3. Vector logo and buyer-owned artwork or design direction.
  4. Preferred paper, structure, finish, ribbon and insert.
  5. Delivery country and shipping requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the metallic floral pattern use our own artwork?

Yes. Supply vector artwork so foil coverage, line weight and panel alignment can be assessed before tooling.

Can the ribbon pull match a Pantone reference?

Ribbon and paper colors can be matched as closely as the selected materials allow and are confirmed with project samples.

Can this box include a fitted jewelry insert?

Yes. Product-specific cavities, card slots and fabric-covered platforms can be developed for the intended jewelry or accessory set.
