Specification First

Match the Packaging to the Food and Conditions of Use

Food Type & Contact Layer

Confirm whether the food is dry, moist, acidic, oily or fatty and whether the printed carton, coating, liner or inner wrap contacts it directly.

Temperature & Storage

State filling temperature, room-temperature, chilled or frozen storage, expected contact time and whether reheating is part of the intended use.

Grease & Moisture Control

Select a barrier only after evaluating oil level, condensation, sauce or steam exposure, desired hold time and recyclability or disposal goals.

Structure & Distribution

Review closure security, ventilation, stacking, delivery vibration, carton packing and the way staff and consumers will open and carry the pack.

Food-Contact Review

Why “FDA Approved” Is Not a Blanket Packaging Claim

For US-market projects, food-contact substances are assessed for an intended use. The applicable food type and conditions of use can include temperature, storage and reheating conditions. Review the exact paper, coating, adhesive, ink system, window and liner rather than applying one approval statement to every possible box.

Confirm the Finished Construction

Ask which components form the direct-contact side and request available declarations or supporting documents for that exact bill of materials.

Confirm the Destination Market

Document requirements and permitted claims vary by market. Share the buyer checklist before sampling so materials and pricing can be compared on the same basis.

Validate the Intended Use

Microwave, hot-fill, chilled, frozen or oily-food use must be supported for the complete construction and the actual time and temperature conditions.

Reference the Regulator

See the FDA guidance on food types and conditions of use and its overview of food-contact substances.

Buyer Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of custom food packaging boxes are available?

Project options include bakery and pastry boxes, burger and takeaway cartons, pizza boxes, sandwich packs, meal boxes, sleeves, paper trays and confectionery boxes. The final format depends on the food, serving method, filling process and distribution conditions.

How are the paperboard and coating selected?

Selection starts with the food type, whether contact is direct or indirect, fat and moisture level, fill temperature, storage time and target market. Paperboard, corrugated board, grease-resistant barriers, moisture barriers, windows and liners are then reviewed as one finished construction.

Are all food packaging boxes FDA approved?

No blanket claim should cover every finished food box. For US-market projects, each food-contact substance or component must be suitable or authorized for its intended food type and conditions of use. Buyers should confirm the exact paper, coating, adhesive, ink system and window or liner, then request the applicable supporting documents.

Can custom paper food packaging be used in a microwave?

Microwave use must be confirmed for the complete selected construction and the intended reheating conditions. Do not assume that a paper box is microwave suitable merely because it is made from paper; coatings, adhesives, inks, windows, liners and food type also matter.

What is the MOQ for custom food packaging boxes?

The standard MOQ is 500 PCS per custom size and artwork. Final quantity requirements can change with the structure, board, coating, window, printing method and finishing process.

What information is needed for a food packaging quotation?

Send the food type, direct-contact layer, filled dimensions or required capacity, product weight, fill and serving temperature, grease or moisture concerns, storage conditions, artwork, quantity, destination market and shipping country. Sample cost, timing and the production schedule are confirmed after the specification is reviewed.
