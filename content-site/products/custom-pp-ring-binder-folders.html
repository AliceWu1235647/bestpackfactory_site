Custom PP Ring Binder Folders — Engineering Summary

  • Custom PP Ring Binder Folders are quoted as a B2B project with MOQ 500 PCS.
  • Available ring mechanisms: 2-ring (A4/Letter), 3-ring (US Letter standard), 4-ring (A4 European standard).
