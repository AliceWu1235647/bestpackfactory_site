FAQ

Takeaway Meal Box Questions

Should I choose a compartment box or a single-cavity box?

A single cavity suits one main dish or items that can touch. Compartments keep rice, sauces or sides apart, but add cost and limit how the meal can be packed. Describe the meal and how it is plated, and we recommend a structure to sample.

Are paper meal boxes suitable for saucy or oily food?

It depends on the inner coating, the food temperature and how long the food stays in the box. Tell us the dish, serving temperature and the time from kitchen to customer, and the coating and closure are checked on samples with your actual food.

How is stacking and delivery tested?

We agree the filled weight, stack height, temperature and transport time with you and check them on samples. We do not quote a fixed stacking or leak result without that test.

Is a takeaway meal box the same as a reusable lunch box?

No. This page covers single-use printed paper meal boxes for restaurants and delivery, not reusable plastic or metal lunch boxes.
