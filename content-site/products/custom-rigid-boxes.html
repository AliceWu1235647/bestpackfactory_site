Application Fit

What are rigid gift boxes used for?

Rigid boxes combine a formed paperboard shell, branded exterior and product-specific insert for protection and premium presentation.

Luxury Gifts

Create presentation sets for seasonal gifting, corporate programs, launches and special occasions.

Cosmetics & Fragrance

Organize bottles, jars, applicators and printed inserts with fitted trays or foam supports.

Electronics

Control product movement with a fitted insert and confirm clearance, cable storage and removal method.

Fashion & Accessories

Package apparel, jewelry, watches, eyewear and leather goods with tissue, pouches or insert layers.

Structure Selection

Choose a Rigid Box Structure

The right format depends on product weight, opening experience, shelf presentation, packing method and freight plan.

Lid-and-Base / Telescope

A separate lid lifts from the base. Full or partial-depth lids suit gift sets, apparel and premium retail packaging.

Drawer / Slide Box

An inner tray slides from a rigid sleeve for a controlled reveal. Add a ribbon pull, thumb notch or fitted insert.

Shoulder-Neck Box

An inner neck connects the lid and base visually, creating a reveal line and a precise premium presentation.

Magnetic / Book-Style

A hinged lid and concealed magnets create a book-style opening. It can be supplied assembled or in selected foldable formats.

Foldable / Collapsible

A flat-pack rigid structure can reduce storage and freight volume when the design and assembly method suit the project.

Buyer Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a rigid gift box?

A rigid gift box uses a thick paperboard shell wrapped with printed or specialty paper. Unlike a folding carton, it is designed to keep its formed shape and provide a more substantial presentation.

What is the MOQ for custom rigid gift boxes?

The standard MOQ is 500 PCS per custom size and artwork. Final quantity requirements depend on the structure, materials, printing and finishing.

Which rigid box structure should I choose?

Choose the structure from the product weight, opening experience, display requirement and freight plan. Lid-and-base, drawer, shoulder-neck, magnetic and foldable rigid boxes serve different presentation and packing needs.

Can you make custom inserts?

Yes. Insert options include paperboard, EVA, EPE, molded pulp, PET trays and fabric pouches. The cavity and removal method are designed around the product dimensions and weight.

What artwork files should buyers provide?

AI or print-ready PDF files are preferred, with vector logos, outlined fonts and 3 mm bleed. Pantone references can be supplied when color matching is required.

How are samples and production schedules confirmed?

Sample cost, sample time and the bulk production schedule are quoted after dimensions, structure, materials, finishes, insert, quantity and destination are confirmed.
