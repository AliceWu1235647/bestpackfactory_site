Buyer FAQ

Wholesale Paper Bag Questions

What is the wholesale MOQ for custom paper bags?

MOQ is 500 PCS per custom size, paper stock and artwork. Wholesale pricing improves at 1,000, 3,000 and 5,000+ PCS tiers. Request a quote with your target quantity for exact unit cost.

Which paper stock is best for retail shopping bags?

White kraft 170-200gsm with matte lamination is the most popular for retail. Art paper 200-250gsm with gloss lamination gives a premium look. For eco-conscious brands, unbleached brown kraft or recycled kraft without lamination is available.

Can I get custom sizes for wholesale paper bags?

Yes. We manufacture to any custom dimension. Provide your product size or reference bag and we will recommend optimal bag dimensions including width, depth (gusset) and height. Die-cut handle position is adjusted to your design.

Do you offer FSC-certified paper bags?

Yes. We hold FSC COC certification and can supply FSC-certified paper bags. The FSC logo can be printed on your bag with your license number. Certified material is available in white kraft, brown kraft and art paper stocks.

How long does a wholesale paper bag order take?

Sampling takes 5–7 working days. Bulk production takes 15–25 days after sample approval, depending on quantity and finishing complexity. Rush production may be available for orders under 3,000 PCS.
