Cotton Tote Bag Structures and Options

This comparison explains common cotton tote choices in the market. The fabric, construction and final specification for your order are confirmed in the quotation and on a physical sample.

Fabric weave and weightTotes are made from woven cotton, from lighter plain-weave fabric to heavier canvas. Weight is stated in gsm or oz per square yard; heavier fabric holds its shape and carries more but costs more. Fabric and weight are confirmed in the quotation.
Flat or gussetedA flat tote folds small and suits documents, books and light items. A tote with a bottom and side gusset stands open and carries boxes, groceries or bulkier goods.
HandlesShort handles are carried in the hand; long handles go over the shoulder. Handle width and length, and the stitching at the handle joint, are set from the load and checked on the sample.
PrintingSolid-color logos and illustrations are commonly screen printed; multi-color or photographic designs often use heat transfer or direct-to-fabric digital printing. Natural or dyed fabric changes how inks look. The method is confirmed in the quotation.
Sewing and finishingHemmed top edge, inside seam finish, and optional details such as an inner pocket or sewn label are confirmed on the sample.
Shrinkage and colorfastnessCotton can shrink and dyed fabric or printed ink can fade when washed. If the bag will be washed, shrinkage and print durability are checked on a washed sample before mass production.

FAQ

Custom Cotton Tote Bag Questions

Are cotton and canvas tote bags the same?

Canvas is a cotton fabric woven tighter and heavier than standard tote cotton, so a canvas tote is a heavier version of a cotton tote. Which fabric is used for your bags is confirmed in the quotation against your sample or target feel, not from the name alone.

Should I choose a light or heavy cotton fabric?

Lighter fabric folds small and suits giveaways and events. Heavier fabric holds its shape, carries heavier items and feels more substantial, at a higher cost. Tell us what the bag will carry and how it will be used, and we sample the fabric weight that fits.

Can cotton tote bags be machine washed?

Many users wash their cotton totes, but cotton can shrink and printed ink can fade. If washing matters, tell us, and shrinkage and print durability are checked on a washed sample; care instructions are agreed from that result.

How are the bag size and print area decided?

Start from what the bag must hold, for example A4 documents, books or groceries. The print area sits inside the seams and below the handle joints, and it is marked on a template for your size before proofing.

What is the difference between a cotton tote and a non-woven bag?

A cotton tote is made from woven cotton fabric, while a non-woven bag is made from bonded polypropylene fabric. They differ in feel, print method, washing and cost. Our cotton, non-woven and drawstring bag selection guide compares them side by side.
