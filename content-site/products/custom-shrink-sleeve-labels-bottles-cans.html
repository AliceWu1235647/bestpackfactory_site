FAQ

PETG vs PVC: Which is better?
PETG is the industry standard for high-quality shrink sleeves. It is more eco-friendly (recyclable with PET bottles) and offers higher shrinkage for complex bottle shapes.

Do you provide roll or single cut?
We provide both. Rolls are for automatic sleeve applicators; single cuts are for manual application using a heat gun or steam tunnel.

Can I get a matte finish?
Yes. We can apply a matte lacquer to give your labels a premium, non-reflective look.
