Which film should be used?

PETG and PVC behave differently during shrinking. The appropriate film and gauge depend on container geometry, shrink curve, application temperature, storage, recycling requirements and destination-market expectations.

Buyer Questions

Shrink sleeve label FAQ

What information is needed to quote shrink sleeve labels for cans?

Provide the can or bottle drawing, circumference or lay-flat requirement, sleeve height, artwork, quantity, supply format and details of the application and shrink tunnel.

Can shrink sleeves be supplied for beer and aluminum cans?

Yes, subject to confirming the can dimensions, artwork distortion, seam position, film behavior and the buyer's application equipment. A shrink test on the intended container is recommended.

Should buyers order rolls or single-cut shrink sleeves?

Roll supply is intended for compatible automatic sleeving equipment. Single-cut sleeves may suit manual or small-batch application. Confirm unwind direction, core, repeat and equipment settings before production.
