Buyer FAQ

Custom Boxes MOQ 500 Questions

What is your minimum order quantity for custom boxes?

MOQ is 500 PCS per custom size and artwork. This applies to all box structures including mailer boxes, rigid gift boxes, folding cartons and display boxes. Lower quantities may be available through digital printing for certain structures.

Which box structure should I choose for MOQ 500?

Corrugated mailer boxes suit e-commerce shipping and product protection. Rigid gift boxes are for premium retail and gifting. Folding cartons fit counter displays and lighter products. Final structure depends on product weight, shipping method and brand positioning.

Can I get a sample before ordering 500 pieces?

Yes. A physical sample is produced after artwork approval and before bulk production. The sample confirms fit, structure, print quality and finish. Sampling takes 5-7 working days. Sample cost is deducted from the bulk order.

What information do you need to quote custom boxes?

Provide internal box dimensions (L×W×H), product weight, box structure preference, material preference, printing requirements (1-4 color or full-color), finish options, quantity and destination country. Artwork files are required after quotation acceptance.

Do you offer lower MOQ for test orders?

500 PCS is our standard MOQ for offset and flexo printing. Digital printing may allow lower quantities (100-300 PCS) for certain folding carton structures, subject to size and finish limitations. Contact us with your specifications for confirmation.
