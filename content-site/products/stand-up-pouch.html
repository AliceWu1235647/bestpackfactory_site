Buyer FAQ

Stand Up Pouch Questions

What is the minimum order quantity for custom stand up pouches?

MOQ is 500 PCS per custom size and artwork. This applies to all stand-up pouch structures including flat-bottom, K-seal and side-gusset variants.

Which material laminate should I choose for stand up pouches?

PET/foil/PE provides the highest barrier for coffee, supplements and pet food. For transparent windows, use OPP/VMPET/PE. For lighter snacks with shorter shelf life, kraft paper laminate is an option. Final material depends on product moisture sensitivity, oxygen sensitivity and target shelf life.

Can I add a zipper to my stand up pouches?

Yes. Press-to-close zipper is standard. Slider zipper and tin-tie closure are available for premium products. Zipper placement and strength are confirmed in the sample.

Do you offer one-way valves for coffee pouches?

Yes. One-way degassing valves are available for coffee stand-up pouches. The valve allows CO₂ to escape while preventing oxygen ingress. Valve position is marked on the dieline and confirmed in the sample.

How long does sampling and production take?

Sampling takes 5–7 working days after artwork confirmation. Bulk production is completed within 20–30 days after sample approval. Rush orders may be available depending on factory schedule.
