Customization and quality approval

Start with the packed product dimensions and desired unboxing sequence. BestPackFactory can then prepare a structural route covering box depth, lid geometry, magnet position, insert retention and accessory order. Artwork approval should identify foil, embossing, debossing, printing and non-printing areas as separate layers.

Before bulk production, approve the complete physical sample with the intended product or an accurate dummy. Check closure alignment, corner quality, product retention, removal force, tissue fit, sticker adhesion, card size and visible color under suitable lighting. Export-carton quantity and protection should be reviewed with the final finish because dark and soft-touch surfaces can be scuff sensitive.

Quote-ready RFQ checklist

  1. Finished box size or product dimensions and weight.
  2. Required quantity and target delivery date.
  3. Logo artwork and reference color.
  4. Preferred insert and accessory combination.
  5. Destination country and shipping requirements.
