Buyer FAQ

Cannabis Stand-Up Pouch Questions

Are your cannabis stand-up pouches child-resistant?

Yes. We offer ASTM D3475-compliant child-resistant zippers including double-lock and slider styles. The specific mechanism is confirmed during sampling. Certification documents are available after the zipper structure is selected.

What makes the pouch smell-proof?

The aluminium foil layer (AL 7µm) in the PET/AL/PE laminate blocks terpene and volatile compound transmission. OTR is ≤ 0.5 cc/m²·day and WVTR ≤ 0.5 g/m²·day. The opaque structure also blocks UV light.

Can you print compliance labels for different state regulations?

Yes. We print compliant label panels with space for THC/CBD content, government warnings, universal symbols and batch tracking. Provide your state or market requirements and we will set up the label panel on the dieline.

What is the MOQ for custom cannabis stand-up pouches?

MOQ is 500 PCS per custom size and artwork. This includes child-resistant zipper, custom laminate and full-color printing.

How long is the lead time for cannabis pouch orders?

Sampling takes 5–7 working days after artwork confirmation. Bulk production is 20–30 days after sample approval. Rush orders may be available depending on factory schedule.
