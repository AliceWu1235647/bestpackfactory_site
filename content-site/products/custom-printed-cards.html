FAQ

Custom Printed Card Questions

Are business cards and packaging insert cards the same specification?

Not usually. Business cards follow a regional standard size so they fit wallets and holders, while insert cards are sized to the box or mailer they go into and are often larger. They can share the same card stock and finish if you want a matching set; the specification for each is confirmed in the quotation.

How is single-sided versus double-sided printing quoted?

Double-sided printing adds a second printed side, and each side is quoted by its number of colors and any special finish. A plain or one-color back can keep cost down. Tell us the colors on each side and the number of designs.

How should QR codes on cards be tested?

Keep QR codes large enough, with a clear margin around them and strong contrast, and avoid placing them over textures or foil. Test the printed proof with several phones before mass production, and use a link you can redirect later in case the destination page changes.

Can cards have rounded corners or be folded?

Rounded corners and custom shapes are made by die-cutting, and folded cards need a score line so the fold is clean. Thick card and some finishes affect how cleanly a fold or corner turns out, so shape and fold are checked on a sample, and what is available for your order is confirmed in the quotation.
