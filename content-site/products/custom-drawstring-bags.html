Drawstring Bag Structures and Options

This comparison explains common drawstring bag choices in the market. The fabric, cord, construction and final specification for your order are confirmed in the quotation and on a physical sample.

Small drawstring pouchA flat pouch with a cord channel sewn along the top. Commonly used for jewelry, cosmetics, small gifts, accessories and dust covers, either as the retail pack or inside a gift box.
Single or double cordA single cord pulls the opening closed from one side. Two cords pulled from opposite sides close the bag more evenly and are common on larger pouches. Cord ends can be knotted or finished with stoppers or tips; the finish is confirmed on the sample.
Backpack-style drawstring bagA larger bag whose cords also act as shoulder straps, used for events, sports and kids. It needs stronger corners and cord anchors, so it is quoted only after the requirement is confirmed.
FabricMarket options include cotton, canvas-type cotton, polyester, velvet-type and non-woven fabrics. Each takes print differently and behaves differently in washing and handling. The fabric for your order is confirmed in the quotation; we do not describe a fabric as organic or recycled from a photograph.
Outer size vs usable inner sizeThe cord channel and side seams take up space, so the usable inner size is smaller than the flat outer size. Send the dimensions of the item, including any box or card that goes in with it, and the pouch is sized around them.
PrintingSolid-color logos are commonly screen printed; multi-color artwork is often applied by heat transfer or digital methods. The printable area sits below the cord channel and inside the seams. Method and color count are confirmed in the quotation.
Color matchingDyed fabric, dyed cord and printed ink come from different processes, so they are matched to Pantone references or swatches on the physical sample rather than on screen.

FAQ

Custom Drawstring Bag Questions

What is the difference between a drawstring pouch and a drawstring backpack?

A drawstring pouch is a small bag that holds and presents an item, such as jewelry or a gift. A drawstring backpack is a larger bag whose cords are also used as shoulder straps, so it needs stronger corners and cord anchoring. This page focuses on pouches; tell us if you need the backpack style and it is confirmed separately in the quotation.

How large can the print area be?

The print area is the flat panel below the cord channel and inside the side seams, so it is always smaller than the bag itself. Send the bag size and your artwork, and the usable print area is marked on a template before proofing.

Which fabrics can be printed?

Common drawstring fabrics such as cotton, polyester and non-woven can all carry a printed logo, but each takes ink differently, and pile fabrics such as velvet suit simpler marks. The fabric and print method for your order are confirmed in the quotation and checked on a sample.

How are the cord color and bag color confirmed?

Give us Pantone references or physical swatches for the fabric, the cord and the print. Dyed fabric, dyed cord and printed ink are matched on the physical sample, because screen colors do not show how they will look on fabric.

Is a cotton drawstring bag the same as a cotton tote?

No. Both can use cotton fabric, but a drawstring bag closes with a cord and is usually used as a pouch, while a tote is an open bag with carry handles. For totes, see our custom cotton tote bags page.
