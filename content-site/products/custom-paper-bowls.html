Paper Bowl Structures and Options

This comparison explains common paper bowl choices in the market. The structure, coating, lid and final specification for your order are confirmed in the quotation and on physical samples.

ShapeRound bowls are the most common and fit the widest range of lids. Square or rectangular paper containers pack more tightly in bags and on shelves. The shape for your menu is confirmed in the quotation.
Capacity and fill lineNominal capacity is usually measured filled to the brim, while a served portion sits lower so the lid closes and the bowl can be carried. Size the bowl from the serving volume or weight, not the nominal figure alone.
Rim and lidLid fit depends on the rim diameter and rolled-rim profile. Paper lids are common for hot food; clear lids let customers see salads and poke bowls. Lid type and fit are confirmed on samples.
Wall structureSingle wall bowls are the simplest structure; double wall bowls make hot food more comfortable to hold. The structure is chosen from the menu item and confirmed in the quotation.
Inner coatingBarrier coatings such as PE, PLA and water-based coatings are used in the market and behave differently with oil, moisture and heat; see PE vs PLA vs water-based coating. The coating for your order is confirmed in the quotation.
PrintingThe outer wall is printed on the flat blank before the bowl is formed, so artwork is supplied on a curved wrap template; the bottom can also carry print. The method and color count are confirmed in the quotation.
Trial fillLeakage, softening and lid seal depend on the actual food, temperature and holding time, so samples are trial-filled with your food before mass production.

FAQ

Custom Paper Bowl Questions

Is the nominal capacity the same as the amount I can serve?

Usually not. Nominal capacity is commonly measured filled to the brim, while a served portion sits lower so the lid closes and the bowl can be carried. Tell us the serving volume or weight per portion, and the quotation names a bowl with room for it.

How do I match a paper lid or a clear lid?

Lid fit depends on the rim diameter and rim profile, not on the capacity. Paper lids are common for hot food, while clear lids let customers see a salad or poke bowl. Send the lid you use or the rim diameter, and the fit is checked on samples.

Can hot soup and cold salad use the same bowl?

Sometimes the bowl body can be shared, but hot liquid food puts more demand on the coating, seams and lid seal than a cold salad. Tell us each menu item with its serving temperature and holding time, and we confirm whether one specification works or two are needed.

Can paper bowls be microwaved?

We do not make a microwave claim on this page. Whether a bowl is suitable for reheating depends on the coating, lid, food and heating method, and has to be tested for the specific bowl and market. Tell us if reheating is required before we quote.
