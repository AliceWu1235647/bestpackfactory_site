FAQ

What is the MOQ for roll stock?
The MOQ is typically calculated by the minimum weight or meters required for a production run, usually around 500 equivalent pouches or a minimum of 200kg.

How do I ensure it fits my packing machine?
We need your machine's specification: roll width, maximum roll diameter, core size (usually 3" or 6"), and winding direction.

Do you offer cold-seal film?
Yes. We provide cold-seal adhesive coating for heat-sensitive products like chocolate bars and protein bars.
