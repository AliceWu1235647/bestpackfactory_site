Folded Leaflet or Bound Manual: Formats and Checks

This comparison explains options commonly used in the market; it is not a list of what is fixed for your order. Paper, binding, finish and the final specification for your order are confirmed in the quotation and on samples.

Folded leafletOne sheet folded down (for example accordion, roll or cross fold) to fit the box. Suits short instructions, quick-start guides and multilingual sheets. The folding pattern and folded size are set by the space in the packaging.
Saddle-stitched bookletStapled through the spine fold; common for user manuals of moderate length. The page count must be a multiple of four, and the booklet opens flat enough to follow step-by-step diagrams.
Perfect-bound or lay-flat manualLonger manuals can be perfect bound; workshop and training manuals often use wire-o or a binder so they lie open on a bench or desk. See our PP ring binder folders for loose-leaf training sets.
LegibilityMinimum type size, diagram line weight and contrast decide whether the manual can be read. They are checked at actual size on a printed proof, not only on screen.
Version controlPrint a version number or date on every manual so it can be matched to the product revision and language. Each language or revision is a separate version with its own proof and quantity.
Fit with packagingThe closed or folded size must fit the box, pouch or tray with room to insert it. Send the inner dimensions of the packaging so the folded size is planned around them.

Patient information leaflets for medicines are a regulated insert with their own rules; see patient leaflet inserts in cartons rather than this page.

FAQ

Custom Manual Printing Questions

Should my manual be a folded leaflet or a bound booklet?

A folded leaflet suits short instructions and lies flat in a small box. A bound booklet is easier to follow when the content runs to many pages or steps. Send the page count, the inner size of the box and how the user will read it, and the format is confirmed in the quotation.

How are multilingual manuals delivered?

There are two common approaches: one manual containing every language, or a separate version for each language or market. An all-language manual keeps one item but adds pages; separate versions keep each manual thin but need clear version codes and packing by version. Tell us the languages, markets and quantity per version.

How are small text and QR codes checked?

Small type, thin diagram lines and QR codes are checked at actual size on a printed proof, not only on screen. Scan every QR code on the proof with several phones and confirm it opens the right page. Final wording and safety content are approved by the buyer.

Can manuals be matched to the product packaging?

The folded or closed size is planned to fit the inner dimensions of the box or pouch you send, and the manuals are packed by version. Whether manuals are set into product boxes as a kit is confirmed in the quotation.
