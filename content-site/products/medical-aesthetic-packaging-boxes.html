Who Orders Medical Aesthetic Packaging?

BestPackFactory manufactures custom medical aesthetic packaging for medspa chains, aesthetic clinics, dermatology brands, and private-label injectable product distributors in the US, UK, Canada, Australia, and EU. Common products packaged include:

  • Botulinum toxin (Botox, Dysport) kit boxes with ampoule trays
  • Hyaluronic acid filler presentation boxes
  • Aesthetic device accessory kits
  • Skinbooster and vitamin IV drip packaging
  • Collagen serum and peptide product presentation boxes

All boxes can be produced with custom EVA foam inserts, velvet lining, or molded pulp trays to hold ampoules and syringes securely during transit.

RFQ Checklist for Medical Aesthetic Boxes

To receive an accurate quote, please provide:

  1. Box dimensions: L × W × H in mm, plus insert type and depth required
  2. Artwork file: AI or PDF with 3 mm bleed, CMYK, 300 dpi minimum
  3. Compliance requirements: GS1 DataMatrix, tamper-evident spec, or market-specific labeling standard
  4. Quantity: Per SKU and total project volume. MOQ 500 pcs per design

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the MOQ for medical aesthetic packaging boxes?
Minimum order is 500 pcs per design. Mixed designs can be combined in one production run provided each design meets 500 pcs.
Can the packaging be GS1-compliant?
Yes. GS1 DataMatrix and GS1-128 barcodes can be integrated into the artwork. Serialization-ready templates are available. Please specify the GS1 prefix and required digit structure when placing your order.
What tamper-evident options are available?
Options include holographic tamper-evident stickers, destructible security seals, shrink-wrap collars, and glued tuck flaps with tear strips. Your choice depends on the regulatory requirement for your target market.
Do you ship DDP to the US, UK, or EU?
Yes. DDP shipping to the United States, United Kingdom, EU countries, Canada, and Australia is available. Import duties, customs clearance, and last-mile delivery are included in the DDP price.
