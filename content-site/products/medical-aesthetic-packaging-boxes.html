What buyers should confirm before quotation

Start with the primary container dimensions, product count, leaflet or accessory requirements and intended presentation. Specify whether the box needs a serialized-code panel, tamper-evident label area, batch and expiry fields, multilingual artwork or an insert that controls product movement.
