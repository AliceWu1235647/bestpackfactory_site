Luxury Paper Bag Customization

Luxury paper bags require GSM selection, handle strength, bottom board reinforcement and premium finishing control.

Buyer Answer Snapshot

  • This page gives practical industrial parameters, not generic marketing text.
  • Use these points to prepare RFQ details before contacting the factory.
  • MOQ is 500 PCS for custom packaging projects.

Material choice

150gsm kraft paper is suitable for medium retail bags; 250–300gsm white card with bottom board is recommended for heavier luxury retail packaging.

Handle strength

Cotton rope, ribbon and twisted paper handles should be tested for pull strength. 12 kgf per handle pair is a useful premium target.

Finishing

Hot foil, embossing, spot UV and soft-touch lamination should be planned with logo size and scratch resistance requirements.
