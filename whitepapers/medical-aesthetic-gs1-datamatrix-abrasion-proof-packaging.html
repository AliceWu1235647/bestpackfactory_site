Medical Aesthetic One-Item-One-Code Packaging: How to Prevent GS1 DataMatrix Abrasion in Transport

Engineering methods for GS1 DataMatrix ECC200 print placement, ISO/IEC 15415 Grade A verification and anti-abrasion medical aesthetic carton design.

RFQ checklist

  1. Confirm target market and regulatory label requirements.
  2. Send size in mm, product weight, filling condition and shelf-life target.
  3. Confirm material structure, surface finish, barcode/QR position and quantity.
  4. Ask for sample approval before bulk production.
Factory Engineering Workflow

Real Factory Testing Process

  1. Confirm code type, GTIN, serial number, batch number and expiry date before artwork lock.
  2. Place the GS1 DataMatrix on a flat panel away from folds, glue seams and carton corners.
  3. Print pilot samples and verify ISO/IEC 15415 grade before surface finishing approval.
  4. Pack samples in the same export carton style and perform rubbing/transport simulation before final approval.

Common Buyer Mistakes

  • Putting the code too close to a crease line, causing distortion after folding.
  • Applying matte varnish over the code without verifying contrast loss.
  • Using small module size on absorbent paperboard without camera verification.

Buyer Decision Tree

  1. Use 300gsm C1S or 350gsm SBS for stable pharma carton printing.
  2. Keep a quiet zone of at least four module widths around the code.
  3. Reserve a white code panel when the surrounding artwork is dark or metallic.
  4. Use camera inspection for projects with one-item-one-code serialization.

FAQ

What is the MOQ?

BestPackFactory standard custom packaging MOQ is 500 PCS. Exact MOQ can vary by material, printing process and structure.

Do I need a dieline before quotation?

A dieline is helpful, but not required. You can send product size, filling weight and reference packaging, and the engineering team can prepare a dieline for approval.

Can the specification be changed after sampling?

Yes. Material structure, thickness, zipper, coating and print finish can be adjusted after sample testing before bulk production.
