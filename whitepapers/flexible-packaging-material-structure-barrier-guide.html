Flexible Packaging Material Structure Guide: MOPP / VMPET / PE vs PET / AL / PE

A barrier-focused guide for choosing laminated flexible packaging structures by oxygen barrier, moisture barrier, seal strength and filling condition.

RFQ checklist

  1. Confirm target market and regulatory label requirements.
  2. Send size in mm, product weight, filling condition and shelf-life target.
  3. Confirm material structure, surface finish, barcode/QR position and quantity.
  4. Ask for sample approval before bulk production.
Factory Engineering Workflow

Real Factory Testing Process

  1. Start with filling condition: dry powder, oily snack, coffee, pet food or aroma-sensitive material.
  2. Select laminate structure and run heat seal trial on the final PE/CPP sealant layer.
  3. Check OTR/WVTR target against shelf-life requirement, then confirm zipper and tear notch position.
  4. Perform vacuum leak test, filled drop test and zipper cycling test before mass production.

Common Buyer Mistakes

  • Using low-barrier PET/PE for aroma-sensitive products.
  • Selecting zipper position before confirming fill height.
  • Ignoring seal contamination risk for powder products.

Buyer Decision Tree

  1. Use MOPP/VMPET/PE for good barrier and premium matte appearance.
  2. Use PET/AL/PE when very strong oxygen and aroma barrier is required.
  3. Use kraft/VMPET/PE when the brand needs natural paper appearance with barrier protection.
  4. Use powder-proof zipper for fine powder to reduce zipper contamination.

FAQ

What is the MOQ?

BestPackFactory standard custom packaging MOQ is 500 PCS. Exact MOQ can vary by material, printing process and structure.

Do I need a dieline before quotation?

A dieline is helpful, but not required. You can send product size, filling weight and reference packaging, and the engineering team can prepare a dieline for approval.

Can the specification be changed after sampling?

Yes. Material structure, thickness, zipper, coating and print finish can be adjusted after sample testing before bulk production.
