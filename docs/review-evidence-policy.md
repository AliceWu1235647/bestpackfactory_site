# Review evidence policy

Customer review text may remain visible only when it matches the original source record. Do not add `Review` or `AggregateRating` structured data for BestPackFactory itself.

Before adding or updating a visible review, retain a private evidence record containing:

- source platform and source URL or order reference;
- capture date and original review date;
- the unedited screenshot or export;
- SHA-256 hash of that source file;
- permission or platform terms supporting republication;
- the exact public excerpt and any redactions.

Keep private buyer details and order information outside the public repository. A public page may use a redacted buyer name only when the evidence record can be matched to it.
