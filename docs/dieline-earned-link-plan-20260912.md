# Dieline earned-link plan

Updated: 2026-09-12

Goal: earn editorially justified links to the free Dieline library by making it genuinely useful. No purchased links, PBNs, bulk directory submissions, hidden exchanges or claims that a link is required to use the tool.

## Verifiable asset facts

- 17 base parametric packaging structures.
- 180 named preset configurations in the current catalog.
- Four export options: PDF, DXF, AI-compatible PDF and SVG.
- Dimensions are generated at 1:1 millimetre scale.
- Cut, fold, bleed and structure-specific technical information are separated where supported.
- No registration, email gate, watermark or payment is required.
- Commercial use is allowed, but a converter must approve stock, creasing, tolerances, seals and a physical blank before tooling or mass production.

These facts are generated from the current code/catalog and must be rechecked after Dieline releases.

## Linkable angles

1. **Design education:** a no-login source of editable structures for packaging-design assignments.
2. **Prepress workflow:** same dimensions exported to four vector/review formats with named technical layers.
3. **Small-brand utility:** dimensions can be tested before requesting a production quote; use is not conditional on buying.
4. **Packaging procurement:** every template now connects to a buyer-ready supplier specification and RFQ checklist.
5. **Structure-specific references:** outreach should link to the exact paper-bag, tuck-end, magnetic-box or pouch URL—not only the homepage.

## Link quality score

Score each prospect 0–2 on each dimension; work only on prospects scoring at least 7/10.

1. Topical relevance to packaging, print, design, procurement or education.
2. Editorial ownership and clear contact identity.
3. Real audience and maintained content.
4. Natural fit for one exact Dieline URL.
5. No payment, reciprocal-link demand, PBN footprint or bulk-submission pattern.

## Outreach sequence

1. Verify the target page and identify one exact missing resource or outdated limitation.
2. Test the proposed Dieline at the dimensions used in the pitch and retain the downloaded files.
3. Contact a named editor, educator or resource owner with one personalized reason the tool helps their audience.
4. Disclose that BestPackFactory manufactures packaging and that the Dieline tool is free without an order requirement.
5. Suggest one exact destination URL and a neutral description; do not prescribe commercial anchor text.
6. Follow up once after 7–10 business days. Stop if there is no reply.
7. Record only live, manually verified links as acquired.
