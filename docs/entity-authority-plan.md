## Evidence rules

1. Publish a claim only when the holder name, issuing body, scope, date and relevant product/material can be checked.
2. Keep brand and legal entity connected on About, Factory, Certificate, Contact and Organization schema.
3. Label first-party statements as company-disclosed information. Label third-party documents by issuer and scope.
4. Do not publish customer names, logos, order details, signed reports or licence images without permission and privacy review.
5. Do not use Review or AggregateRating schema until genuine, visible reviews and their provenance are available.
6. Do not claim FSC status until a current certificate or valid chain-of-custody relationship is verified for the legal entity and intended transaction.

## Assets that make outreach credible

- A permissioned factory video with timestamps and captions for printing, die cutting, lamination, hand assembly, pouch converting and QC stations that actually exist.
- Six real case-study records, one per priority cluster, each containing product, quantity, dimensions, materials, finish, challenge, solution, sampling, production and destination.
- A certificate verification table with holder, issuer, certificate/report number, scope, issue date, expiry/status, official lookup and downloadable redacted document where publication is authorized.
- A neutral China factory audit checklist and downloadable RFQ template that other buyers and industry writers can cite without endorsing the company.
- Technical visuals: dielines, insert-fit diagrams, pouch layer functions, handle/load considerations and DDP/FOB responsibility diagrams.

## Outreach guardrails

- No paid link packages, private blog networks, reciprocal link schemes, bulk directory spam or fabricated contributor profiles.
- No request that a customer use keyword-rich anchor text. A truthful brand or project credit is enough.
- No certificate logo unless trademark and chain-of-custody usage rules are satisfied.
- No “best manufacturer” press release based only on self-awarded language.
- Every earned link should point to the most relevant evidence or product page, not automatically to the homepage.

- Compare GA4 qualified referrals and RFQs by landing page; prioritize pages that produce real quotation activity.
- Use Search Console to review queries, page pairs and cannibalization before any title, canonical, merge or redirect proposal.

- Re-run technical crawl, schema validation, 390/1440 visual regression and manual AI citation tests.
