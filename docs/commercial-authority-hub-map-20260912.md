## Cannibalization rules

- `/products/custom-packaging-boxes.html` owns printed paperboard packaging-box intent. `/products/custom-boxes.html` continues to serve corrugated/shipping-box intent and must not be redirected.
- `/products/stand-up-pouch.html` owns “stand-up pouch manufacturer” and factory/engineering intent. `/products/custom-stand-up-pouches.html` supports custom printing, artwork and finish intent. Both remain live.
- `/products/custom-rigid-boxes.html` owns broad rigid-box manufacturing. The long luxury-gift-box URL owns premium gift-box presentation intent. `/products/luxury-magnetic-boxes.html` remains a structure-specific supporting page.
- `/products/paper-bags.html` owns broad paper-bag manufacturer intent. Existing custom, wholesale and luxury-retail pages remain narrower supporting pages.
- No country-name doorway pages are authorized. Country content requires materially different regulation, logistics or documentation needs.

## Evidence still required before stronger claims are published

1. Certificate files with verification URLs, holder name, scope, issue/expiry dates and applicable product/material.
2. Child-resistant test evidence tied to exact pouch/closure/size configurations. ASTM D3475 classification alone is not certification.
3. Food-contact declarations and laboratory reports tied to the exact material construction and conditions of use.
4. Real factory video showing identifiable production, QC and location context without exposing customer-confidential work.
5. Real case studies with buyer-approved product, dimensions, material, finish, quantity, sample process, challenge, solution and destination.
6. Independent third-party mentions from customers, trade-show listings, recognized directories, trade media and professional education resources.

Do not fill these gaps with generic badges, stock photos, self-awarded labels, invented case data, purchased reviews or reciprocal-link schemes.
