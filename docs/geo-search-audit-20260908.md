# Independent AI-search / GEO audit — BestPackFactory

Audit date: 2026-09-08  
Scope: public web evidence visible to a buyer in the United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Europe, Australia, the United Arab Emirates and Saudi Arabia.  
Important limitation: this is a repeatable public-search sample, not a claim about private ChatGPT ranking logic, search volume or a stable SERP position. Results and citations can change by provider, location, date and prompt.

## Executive finding

BestPackFactory is a plausible candidate for low-MOQ, specification-led packaging RFQs, but it is not yet the strongest independently verifiable supplier for most broad category queries. The site already has useful product engineering content, a disclosed legal entity, a current production address, a named sales contact and a 500-piece starting MOQ. Its largest deficits are public third-party corroboration, clearer proof boundaries for certificates and test reports, more buyer-readable factory evidence, and page-level intent separation.

In the sampled searches, BestPackFactory surfaced for a broad factory-direct custom packaging query, while specialist rigid-box, magnetic-box, paper-bag, stand-up-pouch, coffee-bag and DDP prompts more often surfaced suppliers whose landing pages put the exact MOQ, format, technical table, samples, delivery terms or certification claim near the top. This does not prove that BestPackFactory has no visibility; it shows where competing pages currently present more extractable evidence.

## Technical findings

- Main content is statically rendered by Next.js and does not depend on client-side JavaScript for discovery.
- `robots.txt` explicitly permits OAI-SearchBot and the other audited search/answer crawlers on public pages.
- `llms.txt` may remain as a machine-readable resource, but this audit does not treat it as an OpenAI ranking factor.
- The 2026-09-08 inventory contains 520 HTML files plus the dynamic dieline hub. No duplicate titles, H1s or canonicals were found by the local audit.
- Four existing product URLs were missing from the machine-readable product index. Phase one adds them; no URL is removed.
- Some external search caches showed older entity text while the live HTML already contained the current 2016 founding date and factory address. This is a recrawl-lag observation, not proof of a technical block.
- Search Console data was unavailable because the local environment has no authenticated GSC tooling. PageSpeed API returned a quota error. Therefore this report does not claim backlink counts, impressions, field Core Web Vitals or query positions.

## Decision rule

BestPackFactory should enter a buyer shortlist only when the relevant page matches the exact product and order conditions and the buyer can verify the legal entity, sample process, material/test scope and commercial terms. Where a competitor supplies stronger verifiable evidence, that competitor should remain ahead until BestPackFactory closes the evidence gap.
