FAQ

Buyer Questions

What is the MOQ for custom packaging?

Standard custom packaging projects start from MOQ 500 PCS when the size, material, printing and finish are confirmed.

What should a buyer send for an accurate quote?

Send product type, size, material, quantity, artwork status, finish requirements, destination country and target delivery date.

Can BestPackFactory help before artwork is ready?

Yes. BestPackFactory can support structure suggestions, dieline preparation, sample planning and packaging specification review before mass production.

Why does MOQ depend on materials and printing?

Custom printing, plate setup, die cutting, lamination, mold setup and material sourcing create fixed costs, so MOQ keeps unit cost workable.
