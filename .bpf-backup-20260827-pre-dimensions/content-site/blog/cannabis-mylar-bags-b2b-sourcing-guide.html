For cannabis dispensaries, licensed producers, and white-label cannabis brands, child resistant (CR) mylar bags are not optional — they are a legal requirement in virtually every regulated cannabis market. This guide covers compliance requirements, material specifications, custom printing options, and how to source CR mylar bags at MOQ 500 PCS.

1. Regulatory Compliance: What CR Packaging Means

Child resistant packaging for cannabis must meet ASTM D3475 (US) or equivalent standards in Canada (CCPSA), EU, and Australia. The standard requires that:

  • Less than 20% of children aged 42–51 months can open the package within 5 minutes
  • At least 90% of adults can open the package in under 5 minutes
  • The CR feature must remain functional after multiple open/close cycles

BestPackFactory's CR mylar bags use a press-and-slide zipper mechanism that meets ASTM D3475 requirements. We provide test reports from third-party certified labs upon request.

2. Material: What Makes a Mylar Bag

"Mylar" is a colloquial term for PET (polyethylene terephthalate) film — a high-barrier packaging material with excellent oxygen and moisture resistance. A typical cannabis mylar bag uses a multi-layer laminate:

LayerMaterialFunction
OuterBiaxially Oriented PET (BOPET) / OPPPrint surface, puncture resistance
MiddleAluminum Foil (7–9 micron)Oxygen barrier, light blocking, smell-proof
InnerPE (Polyethylene)Heat sealability, food contact safety

3. CR Zipper Mechanism Options

BestPackFactory offers three child resistant zipper mechanisms:

  • Press-and-slide (pinch) zipper: Most popular. Requires simultaneous pressing both sides while pulling.
  • Double-zipper: Two separate zippers for double barrier protection.
  • Exit zipper (slider CR): Push-button slider with child resistant feature for premium brands.

4. Custom Printing for Cannabis Brands

Cannabis regulations in most markets require specific label elements printed on or affixed to packaging:

  • THC/CBD content and potency warning
  • State cannabis program logo or symbol
  • "Keep out of reach of children" warning
  • Batch/lot number, expiry date, producer license number
  • Universal symbol for cannabis product

BestPackFactory supports high-volume rotogravure printing and digital printing from MOQ 500 PCS. We can reserve artwork areas for market-specific warnings and regulatory information; buyers must confirm final legal, labeling and child-resistance requirements with qualified advisers in the destination market.

  • U.S. CPSC Special Packaging FAQs summarizes the PPPA purpose and the child-resistant and senior-friendly test framework in 16 CFR part 1700.

What the Spec Means for Buyers

Sourcing cannabis mylar bags is about compliance-ready specifications. The structure PET 12µm / AL 7µm / PE 80µm (or MOPP / VMPET / PE for metalized) targets OTR ≤ 0.5 cc/m²·day and WVTR ≤ 0.5 g/m²·day. The child-resistant closure is ASTM D3475-style with certification documentation available for the selected structure. Artwork reserves a compliance area for warning icon, batch code, THC/CBD content and QR panel. Production checks include a vacuum leak test and a 20-cycle zipper test, with seal strength ≥ 35 N / 15 mm and color ΔE ≤ 3.0.

What certification is available for CR mylar bags?
