Kraft Paper Bags vs Shopping Bags: Material Options Explained

Published August 21, 2026 · Buyer guide · By BestPackFactory

Quick answer

Choose the bag material by load, food contact, print quality and brand look. Natural kraft is strong and economical with limited color pop; white kraft and coated stocks print brighter but cost more; recycled content changes appearance and may need food-contact confirmation. Always confirm the exact paper, coating and handle with a physical sample.

Match material to use

For bakery and fresh food, confirm the paper is suitable for the food type and that inks and coatings are acceptable for the intended contact. For fashion and retail, coated or white stocks protect print and improve feel. For heavy or wet loads, review coating and gusset construction rather than relying on gsm alone.

Compare cost drivers

Paper price varies by gsm, whiteness, fiber source and order volume. Printing adds setup and ink cost per color; lamination adds material and time. Ask for the same specification at 500, 1,000 and 3,000 PCS tiers so the cost curve is visible before you commit.

Check supply and compliance documents

Request the material declaration, coating details and any food-contact or recycled-content documentation that applies to your market. Keep the approved paper sample and specification with the order so repeat orders can be matched.

Frequently asked questions

Is natural kraft food-safe?

Kraft paper can be suitable for food contact depending on the specific grade, inks and coatings. Confirm the complete specification for your food type and market.

Why is white kraft more expensive?

Bleaching or coating adds process cost; it also gives brighter print colors and a cleaner retail look.

Can recycled paper look the same every order?

Recycled fiber can vary in shade between lots. Agree a shade tolerance and approve a production standard if appearance matters.

What the Spec Means for Buyers

Kraft versus coated shopping bags is a look and durability trade. Brown or white kraft is the natural, cost-efficient standard; coated art paper adds print pop and a smoother feel; specialty papers add texture. Handles run twisted paper, flat paper, rope, ribbon or die-cut. Printing is CMYK process or Pantone spot; finishes include matte or gloss lamination, hot foil, embossing and spot UV. MOQ is 500 PCS per size and artwork, with RFQ data of W×H×G, product weight, handle, print, finish and destination.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between kraft and art paper bags?

Kraft is natural and economical; coated art paper gives higher print fidelity and a smoother surface.

What RFQ data is needed for paper bags?

W×H×G, product size and weight, quantity, paper, handle, print, finish and destination.
