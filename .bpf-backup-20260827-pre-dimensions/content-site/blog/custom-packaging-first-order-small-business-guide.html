Custom Packaging for Your First Order: A Small Brand Starter Guide

Published August 28, 2026 · Buyer guide · By BestPackFactory

Quick answer

Start with samples before committing to bulk, choose the smallest MOQ tier that gives a viable unit cost, and plan your budget including samples, freight and duties. Confirm lead time against your launch date and keep the design flexible for future variations.

Samples are the cheapest insurance

A sample order costs little compared to a bad bulk order. Validate size, fit, color and function before committing, and keep the approved sample as your standard.

Understand the MOQ cost curve

Unit cost drops as quantity rises, but cash and storage rise too. Compare pricing at a few tiers and pick the smallest tier that keeps unit cost viable for your pricing.

Budget the full picture

Samples, freight, duties and potential rework all cost money. Model the complete first-order cost before confirming.

Plan for growth

Choose a structure that can extend to new sizes or seasonal variations. Reusable structures and stored dies or artwork make reorders and expansion cheaper.

Buyer checklist

  1. Order samples first
  2. Compare MOQ pricing tiers
  3. Model full landed budget
  4. Align timeline with launch
  5. Keep design extensible

Frequently asked questions

How much does a first order cost?

It depends on quantity, complexity and freight; model samples, production and duties for your specification.

Should I order the smallest MOQ?

Compare unit costs across tiers; the smallest tier that supports your pricing is a sensible start.

Can I grow with the same packaging?

Keep the structure and artwork system extensible; stored dies and standards make expansion cheaper.

What the Spec Means for Buyers

A first order should stay within factory-standard specs to protect both cost and timeline. MOQ 500 PCS per size and artwork; E-flute 1.5 mm or B-flute 3.0 mm corrugated at 32 ECT equivalent for shipping; folding cartons on SBS 250–450 gsm. Confirm die-cut ±1.5 mm, color ΔE ≤ 3.0, AQL 2.5 inspection, sampling 5–7 working days, production 2–4 weeks.
