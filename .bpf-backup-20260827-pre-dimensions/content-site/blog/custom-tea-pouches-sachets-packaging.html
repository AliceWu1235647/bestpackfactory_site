Custom Tea Pouches and Sachets: Outer Bags, Sealing and Freshness

Published August 25, 2026 · Buyer guide · By BestPackFactory

Quick answer

Tea sachets need an outer bag that protects aroma and moisture. Confirm the barrier structure, seal integrity, date panel and whether the outer pack is a pouch, box or combination. Validate freshness with a storage test and confirm food-contact documentation for your market.

The outer bag does the protecting

Individual sachets are porous; the outer pack provides the moisture and aroma barrier. Match the outer structure to your expected shelf life and test it with sachets inside.

Reseal for loose formats

Loose-leaf tea in a pouch needs a zipper or tin closure for repeated access. Confirm zipper durability and barrier around the closure.

Gift combinations sell volume

A pouch inside a rigid gift box or tin adds perceived value. Confirm the inner pack protects the tea before the outer box presents it.

Dating and compliance

Best-before dates are common on tea. Confirm the date panel prints durably and that labeling meets destination-market requirements with compliance advice.

Buyer checklist

  1. Confirm outer barrier for shelf life
  2. Choose reseal for loose formats
  3. Design gift combinations
  4. Plan date panel
  5. Request food-contact documents

Frequently asked questions

Do tea sachets need an outer barrier bag?

For meaningful shelf life, yes; sachets alone rarely protect aroma and moisture long term.

Pouch or tin for loose tea?

Pouches are economical and resealable; tins add premium feel and reuse value at higher cost.

How do I keep tea fresh after opening?

A zipper or airtight closure plus a good barrier film; test the open-shelf window.

What the Spec Means for Buyers

Tea freshness depends on the same variables as coffee: oxygen, moisture and aroma. Outer tea bags use laminates such as MOPP 20µm / VMPET 12µm / PE 80µm or PET 12µm / AL 7µm / PE 80µm targeting OTR ≤ 0.8 cc/m²·day and WVTR ≤ 0.8 g/m²·day, with a press-to-close zipper 6–8 mm for resealing loose leaf. Seal strength is ≥ 35 N / 15 mm, dimension tolerance ±2 mm, and multi-color gravure holds ±0.3 mm registration. For sachet overwraps, the same film families apply with a heat-seal-only format.

Frequently Asked Questions

What barrier does loose-leaf tea need?

Loose-leaf tea is protected by the same high-barrier laminates as coffee, with OTR ≤ 0.8 cc/m²·day and WVTR ≤ 0.8 g/m²·day.

Can tea pouches be resealable?

Yes, a PE press-to-close zipper of 6–8 mm is the standard reseal option for outer tea bags.
