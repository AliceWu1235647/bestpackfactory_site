Custom Wine and Liquor Gift Boxes: Buying Guide for Brands

Published August 19, 2026 · Buyer guide · By BestPackFactory

Quick answer

For wine and spirits, size the box to the bottle plus 4-6 mm around the widest point and use a die-cut greyboard or molded pulp insert that cradles the neck and base. Single-bottle rigid boxes quote from 500-1,000 pieces; double-bottle sets from 1,000. Samples take 7-10 days, bulk 20-30 days. For holiday launches, order pre-production samples at least 8-10 weeks before ship date so freight and approvals fit the calendar.

Size the box around the bottle

Measure the bottle at its widest point, including the label and neck collar, then add 4-6 mm clearance per side. Interior height should match bottle height plus 8-12 mm for the insert base. Most 750 ml Bordeaux and Burgundy bottles need an interior footprint around 90-110 mm square or 90 x 320 mm for slim magnum-style tubes; verify against your actual bottle, since punt depth and shoulder angle vary widely.

Inserts that lock the bottle in place

The insert must stop vertical and horizontal movement. A two-piece die-cut greyboard cradle holding the neck and base is the industry default; molded pulp is a recyclable alternative for mid-tier lines. Confirm insert depth tolerance of ±1 mm and require a packed drop test at 60-80 cm. Foam or EVA liners inside the cradle protect labels, which buyers should ask for explicitly on high-end labels.

Windows, finishes and seasonal planning

A front window (PET or PP film, 0.25-0.5 mm) lets the label do the selling, but confirm film grade and whether the design is glued or heat-sealed into the window panel. Foil-stamped logotypes and soft-touch lamination are standard on premium lines. For Lunar New Year, Christmas or wedding seasons, lock artwork 6-8 weeks ahead and place bulk orders 10-12 weeks before the event to protect against freight delays.

Regulatory and duty notes for importers

Alcohol packaging moves through customs with the beverage, and labeling requirements differ by country, so importers should confirm excise and label rules with a customs broker before committing to a design. Ask the factory for material certificates and keep dielines on file; if you change markets later, print-run adaptations are cheaper than a full redesign.

Buyer checklist

  1. Measure the actual bottle (widest point, height, punt depth) before dieline work
  2. Specify a die-cut greyboard or molded pulp insert with ±1 mm tolerance
  3. Request a packed drop test at 60-80 cm and label-protection liner
  4. Confirm window film grade and attachment method with the supplier
  5. Lock artwork 6-8 weeks and bulk orders 10-12 weeks ahead of holiday peaks

Frequently asked questions

What clearance should a wine box have around the bottle?

Add 4-6 mm per side at the widest bottle point and 8-12 mm of base height for the insert. This keeps the bottle stable without letting it rattle or scuff labels.

What is the MOQ for custom wine gift boxes?

Single-bottle rigid boxes typically start at 500-1,000 pieces per SKU; double-bottle sets and windowed formats usually start at 1,000 pieces. Sample lead time is 7-10 days, bulk 20-30 days.

When should we order for a Christmas campaign?

Lock artwork 6-8 weeks before the event, place bulk orders 10-12 weeks ahead, and build in 3-7 days of freight. Pre-production samples should be approved 8-10 weeks before ship date to leave room for corrections.

What the Spec Means for Buyers

Wine and liquor gift boxes are a weight-and-insert project. Rigid boxes from 1200/1500/1800 gsm greyboard wrapped in 157 gsm C1S art paper or 120 gsm specialty paper carry the bottle; EVA foam (20–60 density) or molded pulp inserts cradle it. Corner gap ≤ 1.5 mm, lid-base fit 1.0–1.8 mm. Finishes include matte lamination, soft-touch, hot foil and embossing. Each box passes a 24 h static load test. MOQ is 500 PCS per size and artwork.

Frequently Asked Questions

What insert holds a wine bottle?

A die-cut EVA foam cavity or molded pulp insert sized to the bottle keeps it stable in transit.

What finish suits liquor gift boxes?

Soft-touch or matte lamination with hot foil and optional embossing.
