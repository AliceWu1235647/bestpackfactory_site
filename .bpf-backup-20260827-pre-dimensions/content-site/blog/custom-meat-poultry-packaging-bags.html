Custom Meat and Poultry Packaging: Barrier Bags That Preserve Freshness

Published August 23, 2026 · Buyer guide · By BestPackFactory

Quick answer

Meat and poultry packaging requires a high-barrier film, cold-tolerant seals and reliable seal integrity across the pack. Confirm the film structure for your process (vacuum, modified atmosphere or overwrap), test seal strength at chill temperature, and confirm food-contact documentation for your markets before production.

Decision table

Retail packCryovac-style barrierCold handling strength

Seal integrity is the core requirement

A tiny leak shortens shelf life and creates returns. Confirm seal strength at chill temperature, define a leak-test method with the supplier, and approve the result with filled samples.

Match film to process

Vacuum, modified atmosphere and overwrap need different film structures and sealing windows. Describe your process, line speed and target shelf life, then confirm the structure with a storage and leak test.

Cold and wet handling

Packs are handled cold and wet. Confirm film toughness, puncture resistance from bones and sharp edges, and print adhesion under moisture.

Documentation and compliance

Request food-contact declarations and any destination-market documentation, and confirm labeling requirements with compliance advisors before mass production.

Buyer checklist

  1. Confirm film for your process
  2. Test seal integrity at chill temperature
  3. Check puncture resistance from bones
  4. Verify print adhesion when cold
  5. Request food-contact documents

Frequently asked questions

What film is best for meat packaging?

The structure depends on the process: vacuum, MAP and overwrap each need different barrier and seal properties; confirm with testing.

How do I check seal quality?

Agree a leak-test method and validate with filled samples under cold conditions.

Do I need special inks for cold packs?

Confirm ink and lamination adhesion under cold, wet handling; delamination is a common failure.
