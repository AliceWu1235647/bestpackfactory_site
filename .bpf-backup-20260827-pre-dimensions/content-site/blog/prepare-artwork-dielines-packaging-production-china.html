Print and color verification method

Method for How to Prepare Artwork & Dielines for Custom Packaging Production: Record the approved physical reference, substrate, ink and print process, finish, viewing condition, instrument geometry, illuminant and observer, color-difference formula, measurement locations and acceptance limit.

  • ISO 3664:2025 specifies viewing conditions for critical comparison of prints and reference objects.
  • International Color Consortium Profile Registry links registered profiles to standard printing conditions and public characterization data sets.

Evidence limit: A color value without the formula, instrument conditions, substrate and reference is incomplete. Final approval should use the agreed proof or physical sample and a project-specific measurement record.
