Custom Pet Supplement Pouches: Barrier, Dating and Brand Protection

Published August 23, 2026 · Buyer guide · By BestPackFactory

Quick answer

Pet supplement pouches need a high barrier against light, oxygen and moisture to protect active ingredients, plus clear date and batch printing and tamper evidence. Confirm the film structure, print durability and label area with a filled sample, and confirm compliance documentation for your markets.

Active ingredients need real protection

Light, oxygen and moisture degrade many supplement ingredients. Confirm the barrier structure and test the packed product over the claimed shelf life rather than relying on a generic film specification.

Dating and batch traceability

Plan a date and batch area that prints cleanly and survives handling. Confirm ink adhesion on the film and readability after storage.

Tamper evidence builds trust

A tear notch, seal line or band signals the pack has not been opened. Choose the mechanism that fits your filling line and confirm it is visible and effective.

Labeling and compliance

Supplement labeling is regulated; confirm ingredients, dosage, warnings and claims with compliance advisors for each destination market and keep approved artwork with the order.

Buyer checklist

  1. Confirm barrier for active ingredients
  2. Test date and batch print durability
  3. Choose tamper evidence for your line
  4. Plan regulated labeling
  5. Approve a filled sample

Frequently asked questions

Why do supplement pouches need foil?

Foil or metallized layers block light and oxygen that degrade many ingredients; match the structure to your shelf-life claim.

How are dates printed on pouches?

Inkjet or hot-stamp coding on a dedicated panel; confirm readability and durability.

Is tamper evidence required?

Requirements vary by market; confirm applicable rules and choose a mechanism that fits your filling line.

What the Spec Means for Buyers

Pet supplements need a moisture-tight, puncture-resistant pouch with a resealable closure. A standard factory build is PET 12µm / VMPET 12µm / PE 100–130µm with a total laminate thickness of 120–180 µm, OTR ≤ 1.0 cc/m²·day and WVTR ≤ 1.0 g/m²·day. The heavy-duty PE zipper is 8–12 mm and seal strength is ≥ 45 N / 15 mm. For chewable or powder formats, a slider zipper or powder-resistant zipper can be specified, and a matte, anti-scratch surface finish keeps the pack shelf-ready after handling.

Frequently Asked Questions

What barrier do pet supplement pouches need?

A metalized structure with OTR ≤ 1.0 cc/m²·day and WVTR ≤ 1.0 g/m²·day protects powders and chewables from moisture and oxygen.

Is a matte finish available for supplement pouches?

Yes. Matte, gloss, anti-scratch matte and spot UV window or logo finishes are all available.
