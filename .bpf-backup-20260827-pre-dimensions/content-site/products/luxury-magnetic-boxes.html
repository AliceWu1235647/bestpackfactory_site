Buyer Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the MOQ for custom magnetic gift boxes?

The standard MOQ is 500 PCS per custom size and artwork. Final quantity requirements depend on the structure, material, printing and finish.

Can magnetic gift boxes be supplied foldable?

Yes. Both assembled rigid magnetic boxes and foldable flat-pack magnetic structures are available. The best option depends on the product, presentation target and freight plan.

Which finishes and inserts are available?

Finish options include matte or gloss lamination, soft-touch film, hot foil, embossing, debossing and spot UV. Inserts can use paperboard, EVA, EPE, molded pulp, PET or fabric pouches.

What artwork files should buyers provide?

AI or print-ready PDF files are preferred, with vector logos, outlined fonts and 3 mm bleed. Pantone references can be supplied when color matching is required.

How are samples and production schedules confirmed?

Sample cost, sample time and bulk production schedule are quoted after the box dimensions, structure, materials, finishes, insert and quantity are confirmed.

What should buyers approve on a magnetic box sample?

Check closure alignment, hinge movement, wrapped edges, insert fit, product removal, print color, foil or embossing position and export-carton protection.
