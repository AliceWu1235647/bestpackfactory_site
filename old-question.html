What buyers should decide first

Buyers may request material, food-contact, coating or supplier documents depending on market and product risk. For a reliable quote, the buyer should define destination market, contact layer and required documentation before comparing suppliers or approving a sample.

BestPackFactory treats this as a B2B RFQ question, not a retail checkout item. The factory quote depends on size, material, printing, finish, quantity, carton packing and destination country.

Material, sample and QC points

For custom food packaging, common material and feature choices include SBS board, kraft paper, PE coating, PLA coating, water-based coating, PET/PE laminate, CPP sealant and grease-resistant paper. The safest sample approval checks food contact side, odor, grease resistance, moisture behavior, heat response, print rub, stacking and carton packing.

ask document needs before sample approval This keeps the project tied to real production behavior instead of only a mockup or reference photo.

RFQ Checklist

  1. Send product type, dimensions, fill weight or capacity, and target use case.
  2. Confirm destination market, contact layer and required documentation.
  3. Share artwork status, logo files, barcode or QR code needs, and language versions.
  4. Request 500, 1000 and 3000 PCS tiers when comparing launch and reorder cost.
  5. Ask for sample time, bulk lead time, packed carton dimensions and shipping options.

Common Mistake To Avoid

requesting documents after goods are finished. This usually creates misleading price comparison or sample changes later.
