Grease-resistant coating

Burger boxes, fries boxes and fried chicken boxes increasingly use water-based grease-resistant coating, PE coating or PLA coating depending on market requirements.

Cleaner structure

Buyers prefer clear nutrition panels, QR code area, recyclable material statement and oil resistance test data.
