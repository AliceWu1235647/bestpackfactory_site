RFQ checklist

  1. Confirm target market and regulatory label requirements.
  2. Send size in mm, product weight, filling condition and shelf-life target.
  3. Confirm material structure, surface finish, barcode/QR position and quantity.
  4. Ask for sample approval before bulk production.
Factory Engineering Workflow

Real Factory Testing Process

  1. Confirm market requirements for child-resistant closure, warning icon, batch code and QR code.
  2. Select PET/AL/PE or MOPP/VMPET/PE based on smell barrier target.
  3. Validate zipper opening force and repeat closure after filling sample products.
  4. Check artwork layout for required warning panels before printing.

Common Buyer Mistakes

  • Treating normal zipper pouches as child-resistant packaging without closure validation.
  • Using transparent film when opaque packaging is required.
  • Forgetting batch and compliance label space in artwork.

Buyer Decision Tree

  1. Use aluminum mylar structure when smell and light barrier are top priorities.
  2. Use child-resistant zipper for regulated cannabis retail.
  3. Use flat bottom pouch when shelf display is more important than lowest cost.
  4. Reserve a clean white code panel for QR and batch code readability.

FAQ

What is the MOQ?

BestPackFactory standard custom packaging MOQ is 500 PCS. Exact MOQ can vary by material, printing process and structure.

Do I need a dieline before quotation?

A dieline is helpful, but not required. You can send product size, filling weight and reference packaging, and the engineering team can prepare a dieline for approval.

Can the specification be changed after sampling?

Yes. Material structure, thickness, zipper, coating and print finish can be adjusted after sample testing before bulk production.
