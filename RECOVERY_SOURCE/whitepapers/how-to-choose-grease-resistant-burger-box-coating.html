How to Choose Grease-Resistant Coating for Burger Boxes

A technical guide comparing PE coating, PLA coating and water-based grease barrier coating for burger boxes, fries boxes and takeaway food packaging.

Buyer Answer Snapshot

  • Best for B2B buyers comparing material, process and test parameters.
  • Includes measurable factory parameters instead of vague sales language.
  • Use the tables below as RFQ input when requesting samples and quotes.

Factory Engineering Workflow

Real Factory Testing Process

  1. Run a paperboard incoming inspection before printing: verify GSM, coating side, surface dust and sheet flatness.
  2. Print a small pilot batch and leave the inside food-contact surface ink-free when direct food contact is expected.
  3. After die-cutting, test burger oil contact at room temperature and warm condition. Record staining, edge wicking and bottom panel softening.
  4. Pack filled samples into a master carton and run a 24-hour stacking observation to check whether grease weakens the bottom lock.

Common Buyer Mistakes

  • Using uncoated white card for high-oil burgers and expecting the box to resist sauce leakage.
  • Putting full-bleed heavy ink on crease lines, which can crack after folding.
  • Choosing a coating only by price without confirming Kit value, Cobb60 and local recycling requirements.

Buyer Decision Tree

  1. Choose PE coating when oil resistance and cost control are the main target.
  2. Choose PLA coating when compostable positioning is required and the buyer accepts higher cost.
  3. Choose water-based barrier coating when PFAS-free and recyclable-paper messaging is important.
  4. For fries and fried chicken, request a higher grease target than for dry bakery items.

FAQ

What is the MOQ?

BestPackFactory standard custom packaging MOQ is 500 PCS. Exact MOQ can vary by material, printing process and structure.

Do I need a dieline before quotation?

A dieline is helpful, but not required. You can send product size, filling weight and reference packaging, and the engineering team can prepare a dieline for approval.

Can the specification be changed after sampling?

Yes. Material structure, thickness, zipper, coating and print finish can be adjusted after sample testing before bulk production.
