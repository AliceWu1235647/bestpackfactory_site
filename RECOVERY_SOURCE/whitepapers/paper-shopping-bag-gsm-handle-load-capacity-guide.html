Paper Shopping Bag GSM, Handle and Load Capacity Guide

How to choose kraft paper GSM, handle type and reinforced bottom board for retail paper bags and luxury shopping bags.

  • Best for B2B buyers comparing material, process and test parameters.

RFQ checklist

  1. Confirm target market and regulatory label requirements.
  2. Send size in mm, product weight, filling condition and shelf-life target.
  3. Confirm material structure, surface finish, barcode/QR position and quantity.
  4. Ask for sample approval before bulk production.
Factory Engineering Workflow

Real Factory Testing Process

  1. Start from product weight and retail use scenario before choosing paper GSM.
  2. Test handle pull strength on finished bags, not only raw handle material.
  3. Add reinforced bottom board for cosmetics, apparel, wine and luxury retail goods.
  4. Run a practical carrying test and carton packing test before bulk shipment.

Common Buyer Mistakes

  • Choosing paper GSM only by appearance without load test.
  • Using decorative ribbon handle for heavy products without reinforcement.
  • Ignoring lamination cracking at fold lines.

Buyer Decision Tree

  1. Use 120gsm kraft for light retail goods.
  2. Use 150–180gsm kraft for stronger shopping bags.
  3. Use 250–300gsm white card for premium luxury retail bags.
  4. Use cotton rope or reinforced handle for higher load capacity.

FAQ

What is the MOQ?

BestPackFactory standard custom packaging MOQ is 500 PCS. Exact MOQ can vary by material, printing process and structure.

Do I need a dieline before quotation?

A dieline is helpful, but not required. You can send product size, filling weight and reference packaging, and the engineering team can prepare a dieline for approval.

Can the specification be changed after sampling?

Yes. Material structure, thickness, zipper, coating and print finish can be adjusted after sample testing before bulk production.
